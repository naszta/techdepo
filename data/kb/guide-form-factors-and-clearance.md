# Form factors and case clearance

A case lists exactly what fits inside it. The PC builder checks every one of these limits.

## Motherboard size

From largest to smallest: **E-ATX, ATX, Micro-ATX, Mini-ITX**. A case lists every size it accepts.
Larger cases also take smaller boards, but not the other way round: the Lattice M1 Mini and Tessel
Cube M take Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX, but not ATX. Mini-ITX boards fit every case we sell.

## Graphics card length

Compare the card's length with the case's maximum graphics card length. Even 1 mm too long does
not fit:

- Halcyon HX 7070 Twin (301 mm) in the Tessel Nook ITX (300 mm): does not fit. The HX 7070 SFF
  (248 mm) does.
- Halcyon HX 7080 Triple (336 mm) in the Lattice Air 400 (330 mm): does not fit.

## Graphics card thickness

Cards are two to 3.5 expansion slots thick, and small cases limit the thickness too. The slim
Tessel Sliver ITX takes two-slot cards only, so the 2.5-slot Halcyon HX 6070 Dual and HX 7070 SFF
do not fit it although they are short enough. The Tessel Nook ITX and Lattice Pocket ITX take
three-slot cards, the Micro-ATX cases four slots, and our ATX towers any card we sell.

## CPU cooler height

Air coolers must not be taller than the case's maximum cooler height:

- Breeze Frost 120 (155 mm) in the Tessel Nook ITX (70 mm): does not fit. Use a low-profile
  cooler or a 240 mm AIO.
- Coldmark Peak Duo (168 mm) in the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (165 mm): does not fit.

Tall memory can push the limit up: some tower coolers overhang the first memory slot, and with
memory taller than the cooler's clearance the fan has to sit higher. The Breeze Frost 120 with the
52 mm Lumen Apex DDR5-7200 32 GB stands 165 mm tall, too tall for the Lattice Core 200 (158 mm).
See the CPU cooler guide for each cooler's memory clearance.

## Radiators for AIO coolers

Each case lists its radiator mounts and the sizes each takes (120, 140, 240, 280, 360 or 420 mm). An
AIO fits if any mount takes its radiator size:

- Breeze Glacier 360 in the Tessel Nook ITX (side mount: 120 or 240 mm): does not fit.
- The Coldmark Hydro 420 fits only the Tessel Summit Full Tower.
- The Tessel Sliver ITX has no radiator mounts at all.

## Power supply

There are two sizes: **ATX** (standard) and **SFX** (small). SFX-L units are longer SFX units.

- SFX-only cases such as the Tessel Nook ITX and Tessel Sliver ITX do not take ATX power supplies.
- An SFX unit also fits an ATX power supply bay with an SFX-to-ATX bracket. None of our ATX cases
  includes one, so you need to get the bracket separately, and the PC builder shows a warning. The
  Lattice Pocket ITX takes both sizes and includes the bracket.
- Check the length too. The Ferro SF850L (130 mm) is too long for the Tessel Sliver ITX (100 mm),
  and the Nordwatt Titan 1200 (190 mm) does not fit the Lattice M1 Mini (160 mm).

## Drive bays

3.5-inch hard drives need a 3.5-inch bay; the Tessel Nook ITX and Sliver ITX have none.
2.5-inch SSDs fit 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch bays. M.2 drives sit on the motherboard and need no bay.
