# Cancelling an order

What we can do depends on where your order is. Contact support with your order number (for
example TD-1042) and we take care of it.

## Not paid yet

We cancel the order right away. Nothing was charged, so there is nothing to refund. The order
shows as **Cancelled**.

## Paid, not shipped yet

We open a full refund request for the order. A member of our team approves it, usually quickly,
and then we refund the full amount to your payment method and the order shows **Refunded**; it will
not be shipped. While the request waits for approval, your order shows **Refund pending**. Because
the invoice is issued at payment, you also receive a credit note.

## Already shipped or delivered

We cannot stop a parcel that is on its way. Once it arrives, you can return it within 14 days of
delivery. Contact us to request the return, send the parcel back, and we refund you once we
receive it. See "Returns".

## Already cancelled or refunded

Then there is nothing left to do. If you think something is wrong, for example you still see a
charge, contact us and we will check.

## Can I change an order instead?

We cannot change the items or the delivery address of an order after it is placed. Cancel it (if
it has not shipped) and place a new order with the right parts or address. The PC builder checks
compatibility before you order.

## Cancelled orders and invoices

A cancelled order stays visible in your account under cancelled orders, together with its invoice
and credit note, so your records stay complete.
