# Payment

## How do I pay?

Place your order, then pay with your card. Until the payment is confirmed, the order shows
**Awaiting payment** and we do not ship it. Prices are in euros and include VAT.

As soon as your payment is confirmed, the order changes to **Paid**, you receive an order
confirmation with your invoice, and we start preparing your parcel.

## My payment was declined

Nothing was charged and your order stays in **Awaiting payment**. You can retry the payment from
your order page, with the same card or another one. If it keeps failing, check with your bank.

## I want to cancel before paying

Contact us and we cancel the unpaid order immediately. See "Cancelling an order".

## I was charged twice

Your order page shows every charge and refund in a statement view, so you can compare it with your
bank statement. If you see two charges for one order, contact support with your order number.

We check the payment records. If there really is a duplicate charge, we prepare a refund for it,
which a member of our team approves, and then the money goes back to your card. If our records show
only one charge, we tell you what we see; the second entry on your statement may be a pending
authorisation that your bank releases automatically.

## Is my payment information safe?

We never see or store your full card details; they go directly to our payment provider.
