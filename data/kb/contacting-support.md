# Contacting support

## How do I contact you?

Send an email to our support address, or use the "Contact support about this order" link on your
order page, which fills in the order number for you. You can also use the contact form in your
account.

## What should I include?

- Your **order number**, such as TD-1042, if your question is about an order
- What you need: for example cancel, where is my order, a billing problem, a return or a product
  question
- For technical problems: which product, what happens and what you have tried

One topic per email helps us answer faster.

## Ticket references

Every conversation gets a ticket reference such as **T-7KQ2MX**. It is in the subject of our
replies. Keep it in the subject when you reply, so your message joins the same conversation.

## Who answers?

Many questions are answered quickly with the help of our AI assistant, for example product and
compatibility questions or delivery status. Anything involving money, such as refunds and
compensation, is always decided by a member of our team. If the assistant is unsure, it hands your
ticket to a person.

## How fast will you reply?

Most questions get an answer within minutes. Questions that need a person to decide, such as a
refund approval or a carrier investigation, can take longer; we let you know what is happening in
the meantime.

## If we do not hear back from you

If we asked you a question and do not hear back, we send a reminder. After that, we close the
ticket. You can reopen it at any time by replying.
