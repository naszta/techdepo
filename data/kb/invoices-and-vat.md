# Invoices and VAT

## When do I get my invoice?

We issue the invoice automatically as soon as your payment is confirmed. It is attached to your
order confirmation email and you can open it at any time from your order page.

Each invoice has a unique number such as **TD-2026-000042**: the year, then a sequence number.

## Are prices shown with VAT?

Yes. Every price in the shop includes VAT. The invoice shows, for each line and in total, the net
amount, the VAT and the gross amount you paid. The VAT rate is shown on the invoice; it is
**27 %** unless stated otherwise.

## My invoice is wrong

Tell us what is wrong, for example a missing or incorrect billing address. Invoices are legal
documents, so we never edit or delete one. Instead we:

1. **Void** the incorrect invoice by issuing a credit note that refers to it, and
2. **Issue a corrected invoice** that refers to the one it replaces.

Because no money moves, this is done automatically and you receive the corrected invoice by email.
Both documents stay in your order history.

## Refunds and credit notes

When we refund an order that was already invoiced, we issue a **credit note** for the refunded
amount, referring to the original invoice. A refunded duplicate charge needs none: the invoice
covers the order once, and the duplicate was never invoiced. See "Refunds".

## Billing address

You enter a billing address at checkout, or use your shipping address. The invoice is made out to
that address. If you need a different name or address on the invoice, contact us and we issue a
corrected invoice as described above.

## Can I get a PDF?

Invoices are provided as HTML documents that you can open in any browser and print or save as a
PDF from there.
