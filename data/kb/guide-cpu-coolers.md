# Choosing a CPU cooler

## Do I need one?

Some CPUs include a stock cooler: the Voltaris V5 5600, V5 5600G and V5 7600, and the Helion H3
1210 and H5 1440F. All other CPUs we sell come without one, and a build without a cooler will not
run. The PC builder warns when a CPU without a stock cooler has no cooler in the build.

## Match the rating to the CPU

Every cooler has a rating in watts: the heat it can remove. Compare it with the CPU's **sustained
power**, not just its TDP. Many CPUs draw more than their TDP under long all-core loads, and some
far more: the Helion H7 1470 and H9 2290 are sold as 125 W parts but draw about 250 W.

| CPU | TDP | Sustained power |
|---|---|---|
| Voltaris V5 5600, V5 5600G | 65 W | 76 W |
| Voltaris V5 7600, V5 8600 | 65 W | 88 W |
| Helion H3 1210 | 60 W | 89 W |
| Voltaris V7 5800 | 105 W | 142 W |
| Helion H5 1440F | 65 W | 148 W |
| Helion H5 2250 | 125 W | 159 W |
| Voltaris V7 8700 | 120 W | 162 W |
| Voltaris V9 8950 | 170 W | 230 W |
| Helion H9 2290 | 125 W | 250 W |
| Helion H7 1470 | 125 W | 253 W |

| Cooler | Rating | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Breeze Whisper LP | 95 W | CPUs up to 95 W in slim cases: Voltaris V5 5600, V5 5600G, V5 7600, V5 8600 and Helion H3 1210 |
| Coldmark Classic 92 | 120 W | the same AMD CPUs (AM4 and AM5 only) |
| Coldmark Slim 67 | 130 W | CPUs up to 130 W in compact cases |
| Breeze Frost 120, Breeze Glacier 120 | 180 W | up to the Voltaris V7 8700 and Helion H5 2250 |
| Breeze Frost 140 Duo, Breeze Glacier 240 | 250 W | up to the Voltaris V9 8950 and Helion H9 2290 |
| Coldmark Peak Duo, Coldmark Hydro 280 | 270–280 W | every CPU we sell, quietly |
| Breeze Glacier 360, Coldmark Hydro 360, Coldmark Hydro 420 | 300–350 W | every CPU we sell, with the lowest noise |

A cooler rated below the CPU's sustained power gets a warning: it works, but the CPU runs hot and
throttles under long loads. Examples: the Breeze Whisper LP (95 W) with a Voltaris V7 8700
(162 W), and the Breeze Frost 120 (180 W) with a Helion H7 1470 (253 W).

## Socket support

The cooler must include a mount for your CPU's socket. Most of ours support AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and
LGA1851. The **Coldmark Classic 92 is AMD only** (AM4 and AM5) and does not fit Helion CPUs.

## Air or AIO?

- **Air coolers** are cheaper, quieter at moderate loads and have no pump to wear out. Check the
  case's maximum cooler height.
- **AIO liquid coolers** move heat to a radiator, so the block on the CPU is low. They suit hot
  CPUs and cases with little height. Check that the case has a mount for the radiator size.

## Memory clearance

Some air coolers overhang the first memory slot with their fan. Each one leaves room for memory up
to a certain height; with taller memory the fan has to sit higher, which makes the cooler taller.
The PC builder warns about it and checks the raised height against the case.

| Cooler | Memory height under the fan |
|---|---|
| Coldmark Peak Duo | 36 mm |
| Breeze Frost 140 Duo | 40 mm |
| Breeze Frost 120, Coldmark Slim 67 | 42 mm |
| Coldmark Classic 92, Breeze Whisper LP, every AIO | no limit |

Our Nimbus DDR5 kits are 32–34 mm and the Nimbus DDR4 kits 42 mm tall. The RGB kits are taller:
46 mm for the Lumen Pulse DDR5-6000 32 GB and Lumen Pulse DDR5-6400 64 GB, 48 mm for the Lumen
Pulse DDR4-3600 32 GB and 52 mm for the Lumen Apex DDR5-7200 32 GB.
