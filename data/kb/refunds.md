# Refunds

## When do I get a refund?

- **You cancel a paid order before it ships.** See "Cancelling an order".
- **You return a product** within 14 days of delivery. The refund starts when we receive the
  parcel. See "Returns".
- **You were charged twice.** We check the payment records. If there really is a second charge,
  we refund the duplicate.
- **Your parcel was lost.** If the carrier cannot find it, we offer a refund or a free
  replacement. See "Shipping and delivery".
- **A warranty claim** cannot be fixed by repair or replacement. See "Warranty".

## Who decides?

Every refund is checked and approved by a member of our team before money moves. Our support
assistant can open a refund request for you, but it never sends money on its own. While a refund
is waiting for approval, your order page shows **Refund pending**.

If we cannot approve a refund, we tell you why.

## How is the refund paid?

To the payment method you used for the order. You receive an email when the refund is sent, and
the order page shows it in your payment history. Depending on your bank, it can take a few banking
days to appear on your statement.

A full refund changes the order status to **Refunded**. A partial refund, such as for a duplicate
charge, shows in the payment history without changing the order status.

## What happens to my invoice?

If an invoice was issued for the order, we issue a **credit note** that refers to the original
invoice. Invoices are never edited or deleted. See "Invoices and VAT".

A refunded duplicate charge is the exception: your invoice covers the order once, and the second
charge was never invoiced, so the invoice stays as it is and no credit note is issued.

## How much is refunded?

For a cancellation or a full return, the full amount you paid, including VAT. For a duplicate
charge, the duplicate amount. If a returned product has lost value through use beyond normal
inspection, we may deduct that loss; we always explain any deduction.
