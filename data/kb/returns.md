# Returns

## Can I return a product?

Yes. You can return any product within **14 days of delivery** without giving a reason. This is
your right of withdrawal as a customer in the EU.

The product should be complete, with all accessories, and undamaged. You may open the box and
inspect the product as you would in a shop. If you used it beyond that and it lost value, we may
deduct the loss of value from the refund.

## How do I return a product?

1. Contact support within 14 days of delivery with your order number (for example TD-1042) and
   tell us which items you want to return. The easiest way is the "Contact support about this
   order" link on your order page.
2. We confirm the return and your order shows **Return requested**.
3. Pack the product securely, preferably in its original box, and send it back to us.
4. When the parcel arrives, your order changes to **Returned** and we start your refund.

## Who pays for return shipping?

If you return a product because you changed your mind, the return shipping is on you. If the
product is faulty or we sent the wrong item, we cover it; tell us when you contact support.

## When do I get my money back?

We start the refund as soon as we receive the parcel. Every refund is checked and approved by a
member of our team before the money is sent. See "Refunds" for details.

## My order has not shipped yet

Then you do not need a return: you can cancel instead. See "Cancelling an order".

## The product is faulty

Within 14 days you can return it as above. After that, the 24-month warranty applies. See
"Warranty".
