# Warranty

## How long is the warranty?

Every product we sell has a **24-month warranty** from the day it was delivered.

## What does it cover?

Defects that were not caused by you: a product that stops working, does not work as described, or
was faulty from the start.

It does not cover damage caused by accidents, misuse, modification or improper installation, for
example bent CPU socket pins, a cracked circuit board or liquid damage. Normal wear, such as fan
noise that increases over the years, is not a defect.

Compatibility is not a defect either. If a part works but does not fit your build, see "Returns"
(within 14 days) and use the PC builder, which checks compatibility before you order.

## How do I make a claim?

Contact support with:

- your order number (for example TD-1042)
- which product is affected
- what happens, and since when
- what you have already tried

We may ask you to try a few troubleshooting steps first. Many problems, such as a PC that does
not start after a CPU upgrade, are solved by a BIOS update or by reseating a part.

## What happens next?

If the product is defective, we repair or replace it. If neither is possible, we refund it. When a
replacement or return shipment is needed, we tell you exactly how to send the product and we
cover the shipping.

## Within the first 14 days

You can also simply return the product within 14 days of delivery. See "Returns".
