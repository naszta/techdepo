# Shipping and delivery

## Where do you ship?

We ship to every country of the European Union: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, the
Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy,
Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia,
Slovenia, Spain and Sweden. We do not ship outside the EU, for example to Switzerland, Norway or
the United Kingdom; checkout only accepts an EU delivery address. Shipping is free everywhere, and
the delivery time is the same.

## When does my order ship?

We prepare your order as soon as payment is confirmed. While we pick and pack it, the order shows
as **Processing**. When we hand it to the carrier, it changes to **Shipped** and you receive an
email with the tracking number. Unpaid orders are not shipped.

Shipping is free: the total you see at checkout is the total you pay.

## How long does delivery take?

Delivery takes **1–3 workdays** after the order ships. Workdays are Monday to Friday; weekends and
public holidays do not count. The carrier gives each parcel an estimated delivery date, which you
can see on your order page.

Example: an order shipped on Friday is on its first workday on Monday, so it may arrive as late as
Wednesday.

## Can I change the delivery address?

Not on an order that has been placed: the address is part of the order and of its invoice. Before
the order ships, you can cancel it and place it again with the right address. An unpaid order is
cancelled at once; a paid one is refunded in full once our team approves (see "Cancelling an
order"). Once the order has shipped, the parcel goes to the address on the order and cannot be
redirected.

To correct the billing address on an invoice, see "Invoices and VAT".

## My parcel has not arrived yet

We count the full workdays since your order shipped:

- **Up to 3 workdays:** your parcel is within the normal delivery time. Please check the
  tracking link and the estimated delivery date on your order page.
- **4 workdays:** the carrier is sometimes overloaded and a parcel takes a day longer than
  usual. Please give it a little more time.
- **5 workdays or more:** something is wrong. Contact us and we open an investigation with the
  carrier straight away.

## What happens during an investigation?

We ask the carrier to trace the parcel. Often it turns up, and the carrier delivers it within a
day. If the carrier cannot find it, our team contacts you with an offer: usually a free
replacement shipment or a full refund. You choose what suits you. While the investigation is
open, your order page shows it.

## Tracking

Your tracking number is in the shipping email and on your order page. The order changes to
**Delivered** when the carrier confirms delivery. If the tracking says delivered but you have no
parcel, contact us with your order number and we will look into it.

## Contacting us about a delivery

Reply to your shipping email or write to support with your order number (for example TD-1042).
See "Contacting support".
