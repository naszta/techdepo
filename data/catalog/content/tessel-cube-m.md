## Overview

The Tessel Cube M is a Micro-ATX cube case with a horizontal layout that keeps the graphics card
and CPU cooler in separate airflow zones. It takes Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX boards, graphics cards up
to 350 mm, air coolers up to 170 mm and ATX power supplies up to 180 mm. The top takes radiators up
to 360 mm, the front 120 or 240 mm and the rear 120 mm. It has two 2.5-inch bays, one 3.5-inch bay
and two included fans.

## Who it's for

Builders who want a compact, desk-friendly case with high-end clearances, including a 360 mm AIO
and a long graphics card.

## Pros

- 360 mm radiator in the top
- Fits graphics cards up to 350 mm and coolers up to 170 mm
- Uses regular ATX power supplies
- Compact cube footprint

## Cons

- No full-size ATX motherboards
- Power supplies limited to 180 mm
- Wider than a mini tower

## FAQ

### Does the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple fit?

No. It is 357 mm long and the Cube M takes cards up to 350 mm. The Halcyon HX 7080 Triple
(336 mm) and Aurex Nova 90 (340 mm) fit.

### Does the Coldmark Peak Duo fit?

Yes. It is 168 mm tall and the Cube M allows 170 mm.

### Can I use an ATX motherboard?

No. Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX only, such as the Kestrel B650M Wing or Meridian B760M Forge D4.
