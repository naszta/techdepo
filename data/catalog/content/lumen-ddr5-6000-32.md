## Overview

The Lumen Pulse DDR5-6000 32 GB kit has two 16 GB modules with a 6000 MT/s CL30 profile and an RGB
light bar. It is 46 mm tall. DDR5-6000 CL30 is a popular choice for gaming builds on AM5.

Obsidian (AM5) and Harrier (LGA1700) CPUs officially support 5600 MT/s, so the kit runs at
5600 MT/s by default. Enabling its EXPO or XMP profile sets 6000 MT/s, a mild memory overclock
that boards rated for it, such as the Kestrel B650M Wing (7200 MT/s), handle easily. On LGA1851
it runs at 6000 MT/s, within the Osprey CPUs' official 6400 MT/s.

## Who it's for

Gamers building a showcase PC who want fast, good-looking DDR5.

## Pros

- 6000 MT/s CL30, a sweet spot for AM5 gaming
- RGB light bar
- Two modules for full dual-channel bandwidth

## Cons

- Runs at 5600 MT/s on Obsidian and Harrier CPUs unless you enable the profile
- 46 mm tall; check clearance under large tower coolers
- Costs more than the non-RGB Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6000 32 GB

## FAQ

### What is the difference to the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6000 32 GB?

Same speed and timings. The Lumen adds RGB and a taller 46 mm heat spreader; the Nimbus is 34 mm
and cheaper.

### Does it fit under a tower cooler?

Single-tower coolers such as the Breeze Frost 120 leave about 42 mm over the first slot, so the
nearest module may need the fan raised slightly. AIO coolers have no such limit.

### Does it work with DDR4 boards?

No. It is DDR5 only.
