## Overview

The Coldmark Hydro 420 is an all-in-one liquid cooler with a 420 mm radiator and three 140 mm fans,
rated for 350 W, the highest rating in our range. It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and
LGA1851.

It needs a 420 mm radiator mount, and among our cases only the Tessel Summit Full Tower has one
(front or top). It does not fit the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower, Lattice Flow 500 or Lattice Glass 600,
whose largest mounts are 360 mm.

## Who it's for

Workstation and enthusiast builders in a full tower who want maximum cooling at the lowest noise.

## Pros

- Rated for 350 W
- Large 140 mm fans spin slowly and quietly
- Mounts for every current socket

## Cons

- Fits only cases with a 420 mm mount
- Expensive
- Overkill for most CPUs

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower?

No. The Atlas takes radiators up to 360 mm. The Breeze Glacier 360 or Coldmark Hydro 360 fit.

### Which case should I use?

The Tessel Summit Full Tower, which takes it in the front or top.

### Is it worth it over a 360 mm AIO?

Only for the very highest sustained loads or the lowest noise. For most CPUs, a 360 mm unit is
enough.
