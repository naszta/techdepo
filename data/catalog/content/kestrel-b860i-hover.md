## Overview

The Kestrel B860I Hover is a Mini-ITX motherboard for LGA1851 and Osprey CPUs. It has two DDR5
slots (up to 128 GB, rated to 8000 MT/s), a PCIe 5.0 x16 graphics slot, two M-key M.2 slots (one
PCIe 5.0, one PCIe 4.0, both NVMe only), two SATA ports and onboard Wi-Fi.

It ships with BIOS 5, which supports every Osprey CPU, and has BIOS flashback for future updates.
Its Mini-ITX size fits every case we sell, from the slim Tessel Sliver ITX to full towers.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders who want a current Intel-socket platform with DDR5 and PCIe 5.0 in a
compact case.

## Pros

- Fits every case size
- PCIe 5.0 graphics and primary M.2 slot
- Onboard Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback
- Supports every Osprey CPU out of the box

## Cons

- Two memory slots, 128 GB maximum
- Only two SATA ports
- M.2 slots do not accept M.2 SATA drives

## FAQ

### Can I use a four-module memory kit?

No. It has two slots. Choose a two-module kit such as the Lumen Pulse DDR5-6400 64 GB or the Nimbus
Stratus DDR5-6400 96 GB.

### Does it support LGA1700 CPUs?

No. LGA1851 is a different socket. It works with Osprey CPUs such as the Helion H5 2250 and H9 2290.

### Can I cool a Helion H9 2290 in a small case?

The board supports it, but under sustained all-core load the CPU draws about 250 W. In a compact
case, use a 240 mm AIO such as the Breeze Glacier 240 rather than a low-profile air cooler.
