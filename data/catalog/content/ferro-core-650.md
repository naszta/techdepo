## Overview

The Ferro Core 650 is a 650 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Bronze efficiency and fixed cables.
It has one 8-pin EPS connector for the CPU, two 8-pin PCIe connectors for the graphics card and
six SATA power connectors. It has no 12V-2x6 connector.

## Who it's for

Budget and mainstream builds with a graphics card that uses 8-pin PCIe power.

## Pros

- Low price
- Enough wattage for most mid-range builds
- Six SATA power connectors

## Cons

- No 12V-2x6 connector for newer high-end cards
- Non-modular cables are harder to route
- Bronze efficiency only

## FAQ

### Can it power a Halcyon HX 7080 Triple?

No. That card needs a 12V-2x6 connector, which this unit does not have, and it recommends 850 W.

### Is 650 W enough for my build?

For a mid-range CPU and a graphics card around 200 W, yes, with headroom. The PC builder shows the
estimated load of your build.

### Will it fit a small-form-factor case?

Only cases that take ATX power supplies; it is 140 mm deep.
