## Overview

The Arcwave A5 2 TB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 5.0, reading at up to 12400 MB/s and writing at
up to 11800 MB/s. It roughly doubles the sequential speed of a PCIe 4.0 drive, which pays off when
moving large project files, video footage or datasets.

It reaches full speed only in a PCIe 5.0 M.2 slot. Those are found on the Meridian X870E Crest
(two), Kestrel B650E-I Perch, Meridian Z790 Crest, Kestrel B860I Hover and Meridian Z890 Crest. In a
PCIe 4.0 slot it runs at PCIe 4.0 speed, and in a PCIe 3.0 slot at PCIe 3.0 speed. It runs hot
under sustained load, so use the board's M.2 heatsink.

## Who it's for

Creators and workstation users with a PCIe 5.0 slot who move large files every day.

## Pros

- Over 12 GB/s sequential reads
- TLC flash with high sustained writes
- Backwards compatible with PCIe 4.0 and 3.0 slots

## Cons

- Full speed only in a PCIe 5.0 slot
- Runs hot; needs a heatsink
- Little real-world gain for gaming

## FAQ

### Does my board have a PCIe 5.0 M.2 slot?

Check the board's M.2 slots. Among our boards, the Meridian X870E Crest, Kestrel B650E-I Perch,
Meridian Z790 Crest, Kestrel B860I Hover and Meridian Z890 Crest have one. The Meridian A520M Core,
Kestrel B550 Glide, Meridian A620M Lite, Kestrel B650M Wing, Kestrel B650 Aero, Meridian H610M
Base, Meridian B760M Forge D4 and Kestrel B760 Vector only have PCIe 4.0 or 3.0 slots.

### Will games load faster?

Barely. Games are rarely limited by sequential speed above PCIe 4.0.

### Does it need a heatsink?

Yes. Use the motherboard's M.2 heatsink if it has one, or a separate M.2 heatsink.
