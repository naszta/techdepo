## Overview

The Coldmark Slim 67 is a low-profile air cooler 67 mm tall with a 120 mm fan, rated for 130 W.
It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. It is about as much air cooling as fits in a
compact case like the Tessel Nook ITX (70 mm).

Its 130 W rating covers CPUs that draw up to 130 W under sustained load: the Voltaris V5 5600,
V5 5600G, V5 7600 and V5 8600 and the Helion H3 1210. Hotter CPUs such as the Voltaris V7 8700
(about 162 W) and Helion H5 2250 (about 159 W) will throttle under long loads with it. It is too
tall for the Tessel Sliver ITX, which allows 58 mm.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders who want to cool a mid-range or upper-mid-range CPU without an AIO.

## Pros

- Rated for 130 W at only 67 mm tall
- 120 mm fan stays quieter than smaller low-profile coolers
- Mounts for every current socket

## Cons

- 67 mm: too tall for the Tessel Sliver ITX (58 mm)
- Not enough for CPUs that draw more than 130 W, such as the Voltaris V7 8700
- The fan can overhang the first memory slot

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

Yes. The Nook allows 70 mm and the Slim 67 is 67 mm tall.

### Can it cool a Helion H7 1470?

No. The H7 1470 is sold as a 125 W part but draws about 253 W under sustained load, far above
this cooler's 130 W rating. It would throttle badly; choose a cooler rated for at least 253 W,
such as the Coldmark Peak Duo or Coldmark Hydro 280.

### Will it clear tall RAM?

Memory up to 42 mm fits under the fan, including every Nimbus kit. With taller kits, such as the
46 mm Lumen Pulse DDR5-6000 32 GB, the fan has to sit higher and the cooler gets taller than
67 mm, which matters in small cases; the PC builder checks it.
