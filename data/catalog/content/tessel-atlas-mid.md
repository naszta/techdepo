## Overview

The Tessel Atlas is an ATX mid tower built around airflow, with a mesh front and three included
fans. It takes ATX, Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX boards, graphics cards up to 360 mm, air coolers up to
165 mm and ATX power supplies up to 200 mm. Radiators fit up to 360 mm at the front and 280 mm at
the top. Two 2.5" and two 3.5" bays cover extra drives.

## Who it's for

Most desktop builders: it fits nearly any combination of parts and keeps them cool without much
noise.

## Pros

- Excellent airflow with three fans included
- Fits long graphics cards and 360 mm radiators
- Room for four SATA drives

## Cons

- Mid-tower size takes up desk space
- Air coolers above 165 mm do not fit

## FAQ

### Does it fit a 360 mm AIO?

Yes, at the front. The top takes up to 280 mm.

### How long can my graphics card be?

Up to 360 mm, enough for nearly every current card.

### Does it take SFX power supplies?

It is designed for ATX units. SFX units need an SFX-to-ATX bracket, which is not included, so
the PC builder shows a warning.
