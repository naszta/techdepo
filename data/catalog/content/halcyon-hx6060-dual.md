## Overview

The Halcyon HX 6060 Dual is a 242 mm, two-slot graphics card with 8 GB of VRAM, aimed at
high-refresh 1080p gaming. It draws 165 W through a single 8-pin PCIe connector and the slot, so
it pairs well with a 550 W or larger power supply. Its short length fits most cases, including
many small-form-factor ones.

## Who it's for

Gamers on a budget who play at 1080p, and builders of compact systems that cannot take a long
card.

## Pros

- Great 1080p performance per euro
- Short, two-slot design fits almost any case
- Modest power draw on one 8-pin connector

## Cons

- 8 GB of VRAM limits texture settings in some new games
- Not built for 4K

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

A quality 550 W unit with one 8-pin PCIe connector is enough for a typical build.

### Does it fit in a Mini-ITX case?

Very likely: at 242 mm long and two slots wide, it fits most small-form-factor cases. Check the
case's maximum graphics card length.

### Does it work in a PCIe 3.0 slot?

Yes, with a negligible performance difference.
