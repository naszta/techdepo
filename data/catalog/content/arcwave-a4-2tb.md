## Overview

The Arcwave A4 2 TB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 4.0, reading at up to 7300 MB/s and writing at
up to 6900 MB/s with TLC flash. It holds its speed during long transfers, so it is a strong choice
for a single drive that carries the system, applications and a large game library.

It works in any M-key M.2 slot. In a PCIe 3.0 slot, such as on the Meridian A520M Core, it runs at
PCIe 3.0 speed, about 3500 MB/s.

## Who it's for

Gamers and creators who want one fast drive with plenty of room.

## Pros

- 2 TB at PCIe 4.0 speed
- TLC flash keeps write speed high during long transfers
- Fits every M-key M.2 slot

## Cons

- Runs at half speed in PCIe 3.0 slots
- Gets warm under sustained load; use the board's M.2 heatsink

## FAQ

### Is it fast enough for DirectStorage games?

Yes. PCIe 4.0 drives at this speed meet the requirements of current games.

### Does it need a heatsink?

A heatsink is recommended for long transfers. Use the motherboard's M.2 heatsink if it has one,
or a separate M.2 heatsink.

### Should I buy the PCIe 5.0 Arcwave A5 2 TB instead?

Only if you move very large files regularly. Games load almost as fast from this drive.
