## Overview

The Arcwave S2 2 TB is a 2.5-inch SATA SSD with sequential speeds up to 550 MB/s. It needs a SATA
data port on the motherboard, a SATA power connector from the PSU and a 2.5" or 3.5" bay in the
case. It is silent and much faster than a hard drive.

## Who it's for

Builders who want lots of quiet storage for games and media, and owners of older PCs without an
M.2 slot.

## Pros

- Lots of capacity per euro
- Works in practically any PC with a SATA port
- Silent, with no moving parts

## Cons

- Much slower than an NVMe drive
- Needs data and power cables and a drive bay

## FAQ

### Can I use it as a boot drive?

Yes. It is a big upgrade over a hard drive, although an NVMe drive boots faster.

### Will it fit in a small-form-factor case?

If the case has a 2.5" bay. Check the case's drive bay count before buying.

### Do I need a free SATA port?

Yes, one SATA port on the motherboard per drive.
