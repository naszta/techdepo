## Overview

The Aurex Nova 60 is a 268 mm, 2.5-slot graphics card with 16 GB of VRAM for 1080p and entry-level
1440p gaming. It draws 190 W through one 8-pin PCIe connector and the slot, and the vendor
recommends a 550 W power supply.

The 16 GB of memory is its selling point at this price. New games with high-resolution textures
fill 8 GB cards quickly, and the Nova 60 has room to spare.

## Who it's for

Budget gamers who want a card that will not run out of VRAM in the next few years.

## Pros

- 16 GB of VRAM at a budget price
- One 8-pin connector
- Fits mid towers and most compact cases

## Cons

- Slower than the Halcyon HX 6070 Dual at 1440p
- 2.5 slots thick

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

The vendor recommends 550 W with one 8-pin PCIe connector. The Ferro Core 550 or Core 650 are good
matches for a typical system.

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

Yes. The Nook takes cards up to 300 mm and three slots thick, and the Nova 60 is 268 mm long and
2.5 slots thick. It does not fit the slim Tessel Sliver ITX, which takes two-slot cards only.

### Is 16 GB useful at 1080p?

Yes, in newer games with high texture settings, which can use more than 8 GB even at 1080p.
