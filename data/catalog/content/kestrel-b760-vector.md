## Overview

The Kestrel B760 Vector is a well-rounded ATX motherboard for LGA1700 and DDR5. It has four DDR5
slots (up to 192 GB, rated to 7200 MT/s), three M-key M.2 slots at PCIe 4.0 (the third also accepts
M.2 SATA drives), four SATA ports, onboard Wi-Fi and a PCIe 4.0 x16 graphics slot.

It ships with BIOS 14, which supports both Falcon and Harrier CPUs (Harrier needs BIOS 10), and
it has BIOS flashback. It takes DDR5 only: for DDR4 on LGA1700, choose the Meridian B760M Forge D4.

## Who it's for

Mid-range LGA1700 builders who want DDR5, Wi-Fi and room for several SSDs in an ATX case.

## Pros

- Supports Falcon and Harrier CPUs out of the box
- Three PCIe 4.0 M.2 slots
- Onboard Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback
- Four DDR5 slots up to 192 GB

## Cons

- DDR5 only; DDR4 kits do not fit
- PCIe 4.0 graphics slot, not 5.0
- ATX size needs a mid tower or larger

## FAQ

### Can I use DDR4 memory?

No. This board has DDR5 slots. LGA1700 CPUs support DDR4 too, but only on DDR4 boards such as the
Meridian B760M Forge D4.

### Does it support the Helion H7 1470?

Yes. It ships with BIOS 14 and Harrier needs BIOS 10. Its single 8-pin plus 4-pin EPS power is
enough for the H7 1470 at stock settings.

### Which memory speed should I buy?

Harrier CPUs officially run DDR5 at 5600 MT/s. A DDR5-6000 kit such as the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6000
32 GB (2x16 GB) is a good choice; it runs at 5600 MT/s by default and at 6000 MT/s with XMP enabled.
