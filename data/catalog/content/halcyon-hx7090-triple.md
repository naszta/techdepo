## Overview

The Halcyon HX 7090 Triple is our flagship graphics card: 32 GB of VRAM, top-tier 4K and
ray-tracing performance, and enough memory for serious local AI work. It is 357 mm long, 3.5 slots
thick and draws up to 450 W through a single 12V-2x6 (16-pin) connector.

Plan the whole build around it. The case needs at least 357 mm of graphics card clearance: the
Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (360 mm), Lattice Flow 500, Lattice Glass 600 and Tessel Summit Full Tower
qualify. The power supply needs a native 12V-2x6 cable; no adapter is included. The vendor
recommends at least 1000 W, and with a high-power CPU the 1200 W Nordwatt Titan 1200 is the safer
choice.

## Who it's for

Enthusiasts and professionals who want the fastest card available and have the case and power
supply for it.

## Pros

- Fastest card in our range for 4K and ray tracing
- 32 GB of VRAM for AI and rendering
- One 12V-2x6 cable

## Cons

- Draws up to 450 W
- 357 mm long and 3.5 slots thick
- Needs a 1000 W+ PSU with a native 12V-2x6 cable
- Very expensive

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

At least 1000 W with a native 12V-2x6 cable, such as the Nordwatt Prime 1000. With a Voltaris V9
8950, Helion H7 1470 or Helion H9 2290, choose the Nordwatt Titan 1200. The PC builder shows the
estimated load.

### Which cases fit it?

The Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (360 mm), Lattice Flow 500 (400 mm), Lattice Glass 600 (410 mm) and
Tessel Summit Full Tower (420 mm). The Tessel Cube M (350 mm) is 7 mm too short.

### Does it work in a PCIe 4.0 slot?

Yes, with a very small performance loss.
