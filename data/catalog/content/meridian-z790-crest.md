## Overview

The Meridian Z790 Crest is a high-end ATX motherboard for LGA1700 and DDR5. It has four DDR5 slots
(up to 192 GB, rated to 7800 MT/s), a PCIe 5.0 x16 graphics slot and four M-key M.2 slots: one at
PCIe 5.0 and three at PCIe 4.0, the last of which also accepts M.2 SATA drives. There are six SATA
ports, onboard Wi-Fi and two 8-pin EPS headers.

It ships with BIOS 18 and supports Falcon and Harrier CPUs out of the box, with BIOS flashback.
The Z790 chipset allows CPU and memory overclocking, and the power delivery is built to sustain
the Helion H7 1470 at full load.

## Who it's for

Enthusiasts pairing a Helion H7 1470 with fast DDR5, several NVMe drives and a high-end graphics
card.

## Pros

- PCIe 5.0 graphics and one PCIe 5.0 M.2 slot
- Four M.2 slots and six SATA ports
- Dual 8-pin EPS, Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback
- Overclocking support

## Cons

- Expensive
- DDR5 only
- LGA1700 has no further CPU generations to come

## FAQ

### Can I use DDR4 memory?

No. It has DDR5 slots. For DDR4 on LGA1700, choose the Meridian B760M Forge D4.

### Will the Arcwave A5 2 TB run at full PCIe 5.0 speed?

Yes, in the first M.2 slot, which is PCIe 5.0. In the other slots it runs at PCIe 4.0 speed.

### Does it support LGA1851 CPUs?

No. LGA1851 Osprey CPUs such as the Helion H5 2250 need an LGA1851 board such as the Meridian Z890
Crest.
