## Overview

The Nordwatt Prime 750 is a 750 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Gold efficiency and fully modular
cables. It provides two 8-pin EPS, three 8-pin PCIe, one native 12V-2x6 (16-pin) and eight SATA
power connectors. It is 140 mm long.

With both a 12V-2x6 cable and three PCIe 8-pin connectors, it powers almost any mid-range or
high-end card in our range, whether it uses the new connector or classic 8-pin power.

## Who it's for

Gamers building a mid-range to upper-mid-range PC who want flexibility for any graphics card.

## Pros

- 12V-2x6 plus three PCIe 8-pin connectors
- Two EPS connectors
- Fully modular, Gold efficiency
- Compact 140 mm length fits every ATX case we sell

## Cons

- Not enough headroom for flagship cards with high-power CPUs
- Costs more than Bronze units of the same wattage

## FAQ

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple?

The connector is there, but the card's vendor recommends 850 W. With an efficient CPU, 750 W can
work; the PC builder shows the headroom. For a high-power CPU, choose the Prime 850 or larger.

### Can it power the Aurex Nova 80?

Yes. It has the three 8-pin PCIe connectors the card needs.

### Does it fit small cases?

It fits any case that takes ATX power supplies of 140 mm, including the Lattice M1 Mini and Lattice
Pocket ITX. SFX-only cases such as the Tessel Nook ITX need an SFX unit.
