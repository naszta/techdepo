## Overview

The Voltaris V5 8600 is a six-core, twelve-thread processor from the second AM5 generation
(Obsidian). It boosts to 5.4 GHz, draws 65 W at its rated TDP and peaks around 88 W, so it is
easy to cool and easy on the power supply. Integrated graphics mean the system can boot and
display a desktop without a graphics card, which is handy for troubleshooting or an office build.

It runs DDR5 only, officially up to 5600 MT/s. Faster kits work but run at 5600 MT/s unless you
enable their memory profile.

## Who it's for

Gamers building a new AM5 system who want strong single-thread performance without paying for
cores games do not use, and anyone who wants an upgrade path on the AM5 platform.

## Pros

- Excellent gaming performance per euro
- Low power draw; a mid-size air cooler is plenty
- Integrated graphics as a fallback display output
- AM5 platform with a long upgrade path

## Cons

- No cooler in the box
- DDR5 only, so older DDR4 kits cannot be reused
- Early AM5 boards need a BIOS update before they recognize it

## FAQ

### Does it work in any AM5 motherboard?

Yes, but boards built for the first AM5 generation may ship with a BIOS that predates this CPU.
Check the board's minimum BIOS for the Obsidian family; boards with BIOS flashback can be updated
without a CPU installed.

### Do I need a graphics card?

Not to get a picture: the integrated graphics drive a desktop and video playback. For gaming,
add a dedicated graphics card.

### Which cooler should I buy?

Any cooler rated for 88 W or more, its sustained draw, that lists AM5 support. A single-tower air
cooler keeps it quiet under load.
