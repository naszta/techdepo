## Overview

The Halcyon HX 7080 Triple is a flagship graphics card for 4K gaming and ray tracing, with 16 GB
of VRAM. It is 336 mm long and 3.5 slots thick, draws 320 W and takes power through one 12V-2x6
(16-pin) connector, so it needs a power supply with a native 12V-2x6 cable. The vendor recommends
an 850 W power supply.

## Who it's for

Enthusiasts building a high-end gaming PC in a roomy case who want maximum frame rates at 1440p
and 4K.

## Pros

- Top-tier 4K and ray-tracing performance
- 16 GB of VRAM for high-resolution textures
- Quiet triple-fan cooler

## Cons

- 336 mm long: does not fit many compact cases
- Needs a PSU with a 12V-2x6 connector
- High power draw

## FAQ

### Will it fit my case?

Check the case's maximum graphics card length: it needs at least 336 mm. Mid towers such as the
Tessel Atlas (360 mm) fit it; compact cases such as the Tessel Nook ITX (300 mm) do not.

### Can I use a PSU with only 8-pin PCIe connectors?

No, not as sold: the card needs a 12V-2x6 connector. Choose a power supply with a native 12V-2x6
cable.

### What power supply wattage do I need?

The vendor recommends 850 W for a typical system. The PC builder estimates your build's exact
load.
