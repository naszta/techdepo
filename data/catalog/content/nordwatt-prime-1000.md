## Overview

The Nordwatt Prime 1000 is a 1000 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Gold efficiency and fully modular
cables. It provides two 8-pin EPS, six 8-pin PCIe, one native 12V-2x6 (16-pin) and ten SATA power
connectors.

It is 170 mm long, longer than our smaller units. It fits the Lattice Core 200 (170 mm limit), the
Lattice Air 400, Tessel Cube M, Tessel Atlas Mid Tower and larger cases. It does not fit the Lattice
M1 Mini or Lattice Pocket ITX (160 mm limit).

## Who it's for

Builders pairing a high-end CPU with a flagship card, and anyone who wants a quiet unit that rarely
works hard.

## Pros

- Meets the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple's 1000 W recommendation
- Generous headroom for high-end builds
- Fully modular, Gold efficiency
- Six PCIe 8-pin connectors

## Cons

- 170 mm long; does not fit the smallest ATX cases
- One 12V-2x6 connector only

## FAQ

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple?

Yes. It meets the card's 1000 W recommendation with an efficient CPU such as the Voltaris V5 8600.
With a Voltaris V9 8950, Helion H7 1470 or Helion H9 2290, headroom drops below 20 %, so choose the
Nordwatt Titan 1200.

### Does it fit the Lattice M1 Mini?

No. It is 170 mm long and the M1 Mini takes up to 160 mm. The Nordwatt Prime 850 (150 mm) fits.

### Will it be loud?

Rarely. Most builds use well under half its capacity, where the fan spins slowly.
