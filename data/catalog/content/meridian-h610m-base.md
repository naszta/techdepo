## Overview

The Meridian H610M Base is an entry-level Micro-ATX motherboard for LGA1700. It has two DDR5 slots
(up to 64 GB, rated to 4800 MT/s), one M.2 slot at PCIe 4.0 for NVMe drives, four SATA ports and a
PCIe 4.0 x16 graphics slot. It keeps things simple: no Wi-Fi, one 8-pin CPU power header and a
small footprint that fits Micro-ATX and larger cases.

Check the CPU before you buy. This board supports Falcon CPUs only, such as the Helion H3 1210.
Meridian never released a Harrier BIOS for it, so the Helion H5 1440F and H7 1470 do not work in
it with any BIOS version, and the PC builder flags the pair as incompatible. For a Harrier CPU,
choose the Kestrel B760 Vector (DDR5) or the Meridian B760M Forge D4 (DDR4).

## Who it's for

Office and home PCs built around the Helion H3 1210, where a low price matters more than upgrade
room.

## Pros

- The cheapest way onto LGA1700 with DDR5
- Supports the Helion H3 1210 out of the box
- PCIe 4.0 M.2 and graphics slots
- Four SATA ports for extra drives

## Cons

- Falcon CPUs only; no Harrier support with any BIOS
- Two memory slots, up to 64 GB
- Memory runs at 4800 MT/s at most
- One M.2 slot, no Wi-Fi and no BIOS flashback

## FAQ

### Does it support the Helion H5 1440F or H7 1470?

No. Both are Harrier CPUs, and this board only supports the Falcon family. A BIOS update does not
change that. Choose the Kestrel B760 Vector or the Meridian B760M Forge D4 instead.

### Can I use a DDR4 kit?

No. It has DDR5 slots, and DDR4 modules do not fit. For DDR4 on LGA1700, choose the Meridian
B760M Forge D4.

### Will a DDR5-6000 kit run at 6000 MT/s?

No. The board is rated to 4800 MT/s, which is also the Helion H3 1210's official DDR5 speed, so a
faster kit such as the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6000 32 GB runs at 4800 MT/s. It still works.

### Can I fit two NVMe drives?

Only one: there is a single M.2 slot. Add more storage with 2.5" or 3.5" SATA drives on its four
SATA ports.
