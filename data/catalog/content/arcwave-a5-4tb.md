## Overview

The Arcwave A5 4 TB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 5.0, reading at up to 14500 MB/s and writing at
up to 12700 MB/s. It is the fastest and largest SSD in our range, for 8K video timelines, large
datasets and anyone who wants one drive that is never the bottleneck.

Like every PCIe 5.0 drive, it reaches full speed only in a PCIe 5.0 M.2 slot. Those are on the
Meridian X870E Crest, Kestrel B650E-I Perch, Meridian Z790 Crest, Kestrel B860I Hover and Meridian
Z890 Crest. Elsewhere it runs at the slot's speed. Keep it under an M.2 heatsink.

## Who it's for

Professionals and enthusiasts with a PCIe 5.0 board who need both capacity and the highest speed.

## Pros

- Up to 14.5 GB/s reads
- 4 TB of TLC flash in one slot
- High sustained write speed

## Cons

- Expensive
- Full speed only in a PCIe 5.0 slot
- Runs hot; needs a heatsink

## FAQ

### Is it worth it for gaming?

No. A PCIe 4.0 drive such as the Lodestar Q4 4 TB offers the same capacity for much less, with
almost the same load times.

### Will it work in a PCIe 4.0 slot?

Yes, at PCIe 4.0 speed, up to about 7500 MB/s.

### Does it come with a heatsink?

No. Use the motherboard's M.2 heatsink if it has one, or a separate M.2 heatsink.
