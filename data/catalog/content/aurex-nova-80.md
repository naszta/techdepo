## Overview

The Aurex Nova 80 is a 322 mm, three-slot graphics card with 16 GB of VRAM for 4K and high-refresh
1440p gaming. It draws 330 W through three 8-pin PCIe connectors, and the vendor recommends an
800 W power supply.

Count your PSU's PCIe cables. Three separate 8-pin connectors are required. Units with only two,
such as the Ferro Core 650, Nordwatt Prime 650 and all of our SFX units, cannot power it. The
Ferro Core 750 (four), Nordwatt Prime 750 (three) and larger Nordwatt units can. Its length rules
out cases with less than 322 mm of clearance, such as the Lattice Core 200 (315 mm) and Lattice M1
Mini (320 mm).

## Who it's for

High-end gamers who want 4K performance with standard 8-pin power, in a mid tower or larger.

## Pros

- 4K-capable performance with 16 GB of VRAM
- Uses standard 8-pin PCIe connectors
- Three-fan cooler stays quiet

## Cons

- Needs three 8-pin PCIe connectors
- 330 W draw needs an 800 W-class power supply
- 322 mm long and three slots thick

## FAQ

### Can I use the Ferro Core 650?

No. It has only two 8-pin PCIe connectors and the card needs three. The Ferro Core 750 has four and
costs only a little more.

### Which cases fit it?

Cases with at least 322 mm of graphics card clearance: the Lattice Air 400, Lattice Pocket ITX,
Tessel Cube M, Tessel Atlas Mid Tower and every larger case. The Lattice Core 200, Lattice M1 Mini,
Tessel Nook ITX and Tessel Sliver ITX are too short.

### What wattage do I need?

The vendor recommends 800 W. With a 65 W-class CPU, 750 W is fine. With a high-power CPU such as
the Helion H7 1470 or Voltaris V9 8950, choose 1000 W.
