## Overview

The Nordwatt Titan 1200 is our most powerful power supply: 1200 W with 80 PLUS Platinum efficiency
and fully modular cables. It provides three 8-pin EPS, eight 8-pin PCIe, two native 12V-2x6 (16-pin)
and twelve SATA power connectors.

It is 190 mm long, so it needs a case that takes long power supplies: the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower
(200 mm), Lattice Flow 500 and Lattice Glass 600 (220 mm) or Tessel Summit Full Tower (250 mm).

## Who it's for

Builders pairing the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple with a high-power CPU, and workstations with lots of
drives.

## Pros

- Headroom for the most demanding builds
- Two 12V-2x6 and eight PCIe 8-pin connectors
- Platinum efficiency
- Twelve SATA power connectors

## Cons

- 190 mm long; fits only larger cases
- Expensive
- Overkill for most gaming PCs

## FAQ

### Do I need 1200 W?

Only for the most power-hungry builds, such as a Halcyon HX 7090 Triple with a Voltaris V9 8950 or
Helion H9 2290. The PC builder shows your estimated load.

### Which cases fit it?

The Tessel Atlas Mid Tower, Lattice Flow 500, Lattice Glass 600 and Tessel Summit Full Tower. The
Lattice Core 200, Lattice Air 400, Lattice M1 Mini, Lattice Pocket ITX and Tessel Cube M are too
short.

### Why three EPS connectors?

High-end boards such as the Meridian X870E Crest have two 8-pin EPS headers; the third cable is a
spare.
