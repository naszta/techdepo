## Overview

The Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 16 GB kit provides 16 GB in two 8 GB modules at 3200 MT/s and CL16, the
officially supported speed of most DDR4 platforms. It is a dependable, inexpensive kit for AM4
and DDR4 Intel builds.

## Who it's for

Budget and office builds on DDR4 platforms, and upgrades of older PCs that still use DDR4.

## Pros

- Very affordable
- 3200 MT/s matches what most DDR4 CPUs officially support
- Dual-channel kit for full bandwidth

## Cons

- 16 GB is the minimum for gaming today
- DDR4 only; does not fit DDR5 boards

## FAQ

### Will it work with a Kestrel B650M Wing or another AM5 board?

No. AM5 boards such as the Kestrel B650M Wing, Kestrel B650 Aero and Kestrel B650E-I Perch use
DDR5 slots, and DDR4 modules do not fit them. The DDR5 kit in the same price range is the Nimbus
Stratus DDR5-4800 16 GB (2x8 GB).

### Is 16 GB enough?

For office work and most games, yes. For heavy multitasking or content creation, consider 32 GB.

### Do I need to enable anything in the BIOS?

Usually not: 3200 MT/s is a standard speed on most DDR4 platforms.
