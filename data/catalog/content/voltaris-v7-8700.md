## Overview

The Voltaris V7 8700 is an eight-core, sixteen-thread processor from the second AM5 generation
(Obsidian). It boosts to 5.5 GHz and has integrated graphics. Its rated TDP is 120 W and it
draws up to about 162 W under sustained all-core load, so it needs a real cooler: there is none
in the box.

Memory is DDR5 only, officially up to 5600 MT/s. Faster kits work but run at 5600 MT/s unless you
enable their memory profile. Boards designed for the first AM5 generation may need a BIOS update
before they recognise an Obsidian CPU.

## Who it's for

Gamers who stream, record or edit, and anyone who wants two more cores than the V5 8600 for
multitasking on a modern platform.

## Pros

- Eight fast cores for gaming plus streaming or editing
- Integrated graphics as a fallback display output
- AM5 platform with an upgrade path

## Cons

- No cooler in the box; needs a cooler rated for at least 162 W
- Early AM5 boards need a BIOS update first
- DDR5 only

## FAQ

### Which cooler should I buy?

A cooler rated for at least 162 W, its sustained draw, with an AM5 mount. The Breeze Frost 120
(180 W) or the Breeze Frost 140 Duo (250 W) are good choices. The Coldmark Classic 92 (120 W) and
the low-profile coolers are rated too low.

### Will it work in the Kestrel B650M Wing?

Yes, after a BIOS update: the board ships with BIOS 10 and Obsidian CPUs need BIOS 16. The board
has BIOS flashback, so you can update it from a USB stick without a CPU. The Meridian A620M Lite
also needs an update (to BIOS 15) but has no flashback, so it needs a Quartz CPU installed to
update.

### Do I need a graphics card?

Not for a picture. For gaming, yes.
