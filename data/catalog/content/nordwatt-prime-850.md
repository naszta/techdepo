## Overview

The Nordwatt Prime 850 is an 850 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Gold efficiency and fully modular
cables. It provides two 8-pin EPS, four 8-pin PCIe, one native 12V-2x6 (16-pin) and eight SATA power
connectors. It is 150 mm long.

850 W is the vendor recommendation for the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple and Aurex Nova 90, which makes
this the natural choice for a high-end gaming PC with a 65 W to 120 W-class CPU.

## Who it's for

High-end gamers who want enough power and headroom for a flagship-class card.

## Pros

- Powers 320 W-class cards with headroom
- 12V-2x6 plus four PCIe 8-pin connectors
- Fully modular, Gold efficiency
- 150 mm fits compact ATX cases

## Cons

- Below the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple's 1000 W recommendation
- Overkill for mid-range builds

## FAQ

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple?

Yes. It has the native 12V-2x6 cable and meets the card's 850 W recommendation. With a high-power
CPU such as the Voltaris V9 8950, check the PC builder's headroom; the Prime 1000 gives more.

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple?

Only with an efficient CPU, and without headroom to spare. The card draws 450 W and its maker
recommends 1000 W. With a Voltaris V5 8600 the estimate is about 670 to 690 W, right at the 20 %
headroom line: depending on the other parts the PC builder may warn, and it notes the
recommendation. With a high-power CPU such as the Helion H7 1470 the estimate exceeds 850 W, which
is an error. Choose the Nordwatt Prime 1000 or Nordwatt Titan 1200 for this card.

### Does it fit the Lattice M1 Mini?

Yes. It is 150 mm long and the M1 Mini takes up to 160 mm.
