## Overview

The Voltaris V5 5600 is a six-core, twelve-thread AM4 processor from the Granite generation. It
boosts to 4.4 GHz, runs DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s and ships with a stock cooler, which makes it one of
the cheapest ways to a capable gaming PC. It has no integrated graphics, so a graphics card is
required.

## Who it's for

Budget builders and upgraders who already own DDR4 memory or an AM4 motherboard and want solid
1080p gaming performance for little money.

## Pros

- Very low price for six modern cores
- Stock cooler included
- Reuses affordable DDR4 memory and AM4 boards

## Cons

- No integrated graphics; a graphics card is mandatory
- AM4 is the end of its upgrade line
- DDR4 only

## FAQ

### Can I use it without a graphics card?

No. The V5 5600 has no integrated graphics, so the system will not display anything without a
dedicated graphics card.

### Is the bundled cooler good enough?

Yes, for stock settings. It keeps the chip within limits at a moderate noise level; a tower
cooler makes it quieter.

### Will it fit an AM5 motherboard?

No. It is an AM4 processor and needs an AM4 board with DDR4 memory.
