## Overview

The Aurex Nova 70 is a 287 mm, 2.5-slot graphics card with 16 GB of VRAM for high-refresh 1440p
gaming. It draws 263 W through two 8-pin PCIe connectors, and the vendor recommends a 700 W power
supply.

It is the most powerful card in our range that uses classic 8-pin power and still fits the Tessel
Nook ITX (300 mm). It does not fit the Tessel Sliver ITX, which takes cards up to 285 mm.

## Who it's for

1440p gamers who want 16 GB of VRAM and a card that works with power supplies without 12V-2x6.

## Pros

- 16 GB of VRAM
- Strong 1440p performance
- Standard 8-pin power

## Cons

- 263 W draw needs a 700 W-class power supply
- 287 mm is 2 mm too long for the Tessel Sliver ITX

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

The vendor recommends 700 W with two 8-pin PCIe connectors, such as the Ferro Core 750 or Nordwatt
Prime 750. With an efficient CPU such as the Voltaris V5 8600, a 650 W unit is enough: the
estimate leaves more than 20 % headroom, and the PC builder only adds a note that it is below the
700 W recommendation. A 550 W unit is too small with a high-power CPU such as the Helion H7 1470,
and the PC builder flags it.

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

Yes, with 13 mm to spare: the Nook takes up to 300 mm. Power it with an SFX unit that has two 8-pin
PCIe connectors: the Ferro SF750 or SF850L meet the 700 W recommendation, and the Ferro SF600 works
with an efficient CPU, with a note that it is below the recommendation.

### Nova 70 or Halcyon HX 7070 Twin?

Similar performance. The Nova 70 has more VRAM and uses 8-pin power; the HX 7070 needs a 12V-2x6
cable and has better ray tracing.
