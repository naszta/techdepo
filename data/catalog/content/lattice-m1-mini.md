## Overview

The Lattice M1 Mini is a compact mini tower for Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX boards. Despite its size it
takes graphics cards up to 320 mm, air coolers up to 160 mm and standard ATX power supplies up to
160 mm long. Radiators fit in the front (up to 280 mm), top (120 or 240 mm) and rear (120 mm). It
has two 2.5-inch bays, one 3.5-inch bay and one included fan.

It does not take full-size ATX motherboards, and long power supplies such as the Nordwatt Prime
1000 (170 mm) and Nordwatt Titan 1200 (190 mm) do not fit.

## Who it's for

Budget builders who want a smaller PC than a mid tower without the constraints of a Mini-ITX case.

## Pros

- Compact footprint at a budget price
- Uses regular ATX power supplies
- Front mount takes a 280 mm radiator

## Cons

- No full-size ATX motherboards
- Power supplies limited to 160 mm
- Only one fan included

## FAQ

### Does the Kestrel B550 Glide fit?

No. The Glide is an ATX board, and the M1 Mini takes Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX only. The Meridian
A520M Core is the Micro-ATX AM4 alternative.

### Which power supplies fit?

ATX units up to 160 mm long: the Ferro Core 550, 650 and 750 and the Nordwatt Prime 650, 750 and
850. The Nordwatt Prime 1000 and Titan 1200 are too long.

### Can I fit a 360 mm AIO?

No. The front mount takes up to 280 mm, such as the Coldmark Hydro 280.
