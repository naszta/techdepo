## Overview

The Ferro SF600 is a 600 W SFX power supply with 80 PLUS Gold efficiency and fully modular cables.
At 100 mm long, it fits every SFX case we sell. It provides one 8-pin EPS, two 8-pin PCIe and four
SATA power connectors. It has no 12V-2x6 connector.

It is a good match for compact builds with a 65 W-class CPU and a graphics card on 8-pin power,
such as the Halcyon HX 6070 Dual or Aurex Nova 70. Their makers recommend 650 W and 700 W, so the
PC builder adds a note that the SF600 is below the recommendation; with a CPU such as the Voltaris
V5 8600 the estimated load still leaves more than 20 % headroom.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders on a budget with a mid-range card that uses 8-pin power.

## Pros

- Compact 100 mm SFX size
- Fully modular, Gold efficiency
- Two PCIe 8-pin connectors

## Cons

- No 12V-2x6 connector
- Not enough for high-power CPUs with big cards
- Only one EPS connector

## FAQ

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7070 SFF?

No. The HX 7070 SFF needs a 12V-2x6 connector, which this unit does not have. The Ferro SF750 has
one and is also 100 mm long.

### Does it fit the Tessel Sliver ITX?

Yes. The Sliver takes SFX units up to 100 mm.

### Does it fit an ATX case?

Only with an SFX-to-ATX bracket. Our ATX cases do not include one, so the PC builder shows a
warning. The Lattice Pocket ITX includes one. For an ATX case, an ATX unit is the simpler choice.
