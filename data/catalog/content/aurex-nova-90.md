## Overview

The Aurex Nova 90 is a 340 mm, three-slot graphics card with 24 GB of VRAM. It is built for 4K
gaming, GPU rendering and running local AI models that need lots of memory. It draws 355 W through
three 8-pin PCIe connectors, and the vendor recommends an 850 W power supply.

It needs a case with at least 340 mm of graphics card clearance, such as the Tessel Cube M (350 mm),
the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (360 mm) or the Lattice Flow 500 (400 mm). It also needs a power supply
with three 8-pin PCIe connectors.

## Who it's for

4K gamers and creators who want 24 GB of VRAM without moving to a 12V-2x6 power supply.

## Pros

- 24 GB of VRAM for 4K, rendering and AI
- Standard 8-pin PCIe power
- Strong 4K performance

## Cons

- 340 mm long; needs a large case
- Needs three 8-pin PCIe connectors and an 850 W-class PSU
- High power draw

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

The vendor recommends 850 W with three 8-pin PCIe connectors, such as the Nordwatt Prime 850. With
a high-power CPU such as the Voltaris V9 8950 or Helion H7 1470, choose the Nordwatt Prime 1000.

### Does it fit the Lattice Air 400?

No. The Air 400 takes cards up to 330 mm. Choose the Lattice Flow 500 or Tessel Atlas Mid Tower.

### Why choose it over the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple?

More VRAM (24 GB vs 16 GB) and standard 8-pin power. The HX 7080 is faster in ray tracing.
