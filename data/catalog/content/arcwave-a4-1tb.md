## Overview

The Arcwave A4 1 TB is a M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 4.0 with sequential reads up to 7000 MB/s
and writes up to 6000 MB/s. It installs in an M-key M.2 slot without any cables. In a PCIe 3.0
slot it still works, at up to roughly 3500 MB/s.

## Who it's for

Anyone building a new PC who wants a fast boot drive with room for the operating system,
applications and a handful of games.

## Pros

- Very fast PCIe 4.0 performance
- No cables: mounts directly on the motherboard
- Good value per gigabyte

## Cons

- 1 TB fills quickly with modern games
- Needs a free M-key M.2 slot

## FAQ

### Does it work in a PCIe 3.0 M.2 slot?

Yes. PCIe is backwards compatible; the drive runs at PCIe 3.0 speeds, still several times faster
than a SATA SSD.

### Do I need a heatsink?

Most motherboards include an M.2 heatsink. Without one the drive can slow down during very long
transfers, but everyday use is unaffected.

### Does it use a SATA port?

No. It connects through the M.2 slot only.
