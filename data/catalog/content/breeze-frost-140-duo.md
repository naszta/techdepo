## Overview

The Breeze Frost 140 Duo is a dual-tower air cooler with two 140 mm fans, 158 mm tall and rated
for 250 W. It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. It cools CPUs that draw up to
250 W, such as the Voltaris V7 8700, V9 8950 and Helion H9 2290, without the pump noise of an AIO.
The Helion H7 1470 (about 253 W) is just above its rating; choose the Coldmark Peak Duo for it.

At 158 mm, it fits every tower we sell, including the Lattice Core 200 (158 mm limit), as well as
the Lattice Pocket ITX. It does not fit the Tessel Nook ITX or Tessel Sliver ITX.

## Who it's for

Builders with a high-end CPU who prefer the simplicity and longevity of air cooling.

## Pros

- Rated for 250 W

## Cons

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Lattice Core 200?

Yes, exactly: the Core 200 allows 158 mm and the Frost 140 Duo is 158 mm tall.

### Is it enough for the Voltaris V9 8950?

Yes. The V9 8950 draws up to about 230 W under sustained load and this cooler is rated for 250 W.

### Does it clear tall memory?

Kits up to 40 mm fit under the front fan, including every Nimbus DDR5 kit (32 to 34 mm).
With taller kits, such as the 42 mm Nimbus DDR4 kits, the 46 mm Lumen Pulse DDR5-6000 32 GB
or the 52 mm Lumen Apex DDR5-7200 32 GB, raise the front fan; the cooler then stands taller than
158 mm, and the PC builder checks it against the case.
