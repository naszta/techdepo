## Overview

The Halcyon HX 7070 Twin is a 301 mm, 2.5-slot graphics card with 12 GB of VRAM for high-refresh
1440p gaming and ray tracing. It draws 250 W through a single 12V-2x6 (16-pin) connector, so it
needs a power supply with a native 12V-2x6 cable; no adapter is included. The vendor recommends
700 W.

Mind the length in compact cases. At 301 mm it is 1 mm too long for the Tessel Nook ITX, which
takes cards up to 300 mm. For the Nook, choose the Halcyon HX 7070 SFF: the same graphics chip in a
248 mm body. It fits every mid tower we sell.

## Who it's for

1440p gamers with a mid tower and a modern ATX 3 power supply.

## Pros

- Strong 1440p and ray-tracing performance
- One tidy 12V-2x6 cable
- Quieter twin-fan cooler than the SFF version

## Cons

- Needs a PSU with a native 12V-2x6 cable
- 301 mm: 1 mm too long for the Tessel Nook ITX
- 12 GB of VRAM

## FAQ

### Can I use it with the Ferro Core 650?

No. The Ferro Core 650 has no 12V-2x6 connector. The Nordwatt Prime 650 or Prime 750 are the
closest alternatives with a native 12V-2x6 cable.

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

No, by 1 mm: the card is 301 mm and the Nook's limit is 300 mm. The Halcyon HX 7070 SFF (248 mm)
fits.

### What power supply wattage do I need?

The vendor recommends 700 W. A 650 W unit with 12V-2x6 works with an efficient CPU, but a
high-power CPU such as the Voltaris V9 8950 leaves too little headroom; the PC builder warns.
