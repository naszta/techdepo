## Overview

The Arcwave A3 1 TB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 3.0, reading at up to 3500 MB/s and writing at
up to 3000 MB/s. It is a quick, affordable system drive with enough room for the operating system,
applications and a handful of games.

It works in any M-key M.2 slot and runs at its PCIe 3.0 speed in Gen 4 and Gen 5 slots. On boards
whose M.2 slot is PCIe 3.0, such as the Meridian A520M Core, it delivers the full speed that slot
can give.

## Who it's for

Budget builds and upgrades of older PCs with PCIe 3.0 M.2 slots.

## Pros

- 1 TB at a budget price
- Full speed in PCIe 3.0 slots
- Fits every M-key M.2 slot

## Cons

- Gen 4 drives such as the Arcwave A4 1 TB load large files faster
- No heatsink included

## FAQ

### Should I buy this or the Arcwave A4 1 TB?

On a PCIe 3.0 board, buy this one: a Gen 4 drive would run at Gen 3 speed anyway. On a PCIe 4.0
or 5.0 board, the A4 is noticeably faster for large transfers.

### Does it need a heatsink?

No. PCIe 3.0 drives stay cool. Use the board's M.2 heatsink if it has one.

### Does it work in a laptop?

Yes, if the laptop has an M.2 2280 M-key slot with NVMe support.
