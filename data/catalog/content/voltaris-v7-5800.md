## Overview

The Voltaris V7 5800 is the eight-core, sixteen-thread top of the Granite (AM4) line. It boosts
to 4.7 GHz and runs DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s. It is rated for a 105 W TDP and draws up to about
142 W under sustained all-core load, so plan for a proper tower cooler.

There is no cooler in the box and no integrated graphics: a build needs both a CPU cooler and a
graphics card. On an existing AM4 board it drops straight in, which makes it a cheap way to
extend the life of a DDR4 system rather than replacing board and memory.

## Who it's for

AM4 owners who want more cores for gaming, streaming or compiling without moving to a new
platform, and budget builders who already own DDR4 memory.

## Pros

- Eight cores and sixteen threads on an affordable platform
- Drop-in upgrade for AM4 boards with DDR4
- Strong gaming performance at 1080p and 1440p

## Cons

- No cooler in the box
- No integrated graphics; a graphics card is mandatory
- 142 W sustained draw rules out compact and low-profile coolers
- AM4 is the end of its upgrade line

## FAQ

### Which cooler should I buy?

A cooler rated for at least 142 W, its sustained draw, with an AM4 mount, for example the Breeze
Frost 120 (180 W). The Coldmark Classic 92 (120 W), Coldmark Slim 67 (130 W) and Breeze Whisper LP
(95 W) are rated too low for it.

### Will it work in my B550 board?

Yes. AM4 boards such as the Kestrel B550 Glide and the Meridian A520M Core support the Granite
family with the BIOS they ship with.

### Do I need a graphics card?

Yes. Without integrated graphics, the system cannot show a picture until a graphics card is
installed.
