## Overview

The Coldmark Hydro 280 is an all-in-one liquid cooler with a 280 mm radiator and two slow-spinning
140 mm fans, rated for 280 W. It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. The larger fans
move the same air as a 360 mm unit's three at lower speed, which keeps it quiet with high-power
CPUs.

It needs a 280 mm radiator mount: the Lattice M1 Mini (front), Lattice Air 400 (front), Tessel Atlas
Mid Tower (front or top), Lattice Pocket ITX (front), Tessel Cube M (top), Lattice Flow 500, Lattice
Glass 600 (top) and Tessel Summit Full Tower all have one. The Lattice Core 200, Tessel Nook ITX and
Tessel Sliver ITX do not.

## Who it's for

Builders with high-power CPUs such as the Helion H7 1470 or Voltaris V9 8950 who want a quiet
system.

## Pros

- Rated for 280 W
- Quiet 140 mm fans
- Fits many compact cases that cannot take a 360 mm radiator

## Cons

- Needs a 280 mm mount
- Pump adds a faint hum

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Lattice Pocket ITX?

Yes, in the front mount. That makes the Pocket ITX a good small home for a high-power CPU.

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

No. The Nook's side mount takes 120 mm or 240 mm radiators only. Use the Breeze Glacier 240.

### Is it enough for the Helion H7 1470?

Yes. It is rated for 280 W, above the H7 1470's 253 W sustained draw.
