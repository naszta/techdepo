## Overview

The Meridian Z890 Crest is a flagship ATX motherboard for LGA1851 and Osprey CPUs. It has four DDR5
slots (up to 256 GB, rated to 8800 MT/s), a PCIe 5.0 x16 graphics slot and five M-key M.2 slots:
one at PCIe 5.0 and four at PCIe 4.0, the last of which also accepts M.2 SATA drives. There are four
SATA ports, onboard Wi-Fi and two 8-pin EPS headers.

It ships with BIOS 10 and supports every Osprey CPU, with BIOS flashback. The Z890 chipset allows
CPU and memory overclocking.

## Who it's for

Workstation and enthusiast builders who pair a Helion H9 2290 with lots of fast storage and
memory.

## Pros

- Five M.2 slots, including one at PCIe 5.0
- Rated for DDR5 up to 8800 MT/s
- PCIe 5.0 graphics slot
- Dual 8-pin EPS, Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback

## Cons

- Expensive
- DDR5 only
- Only four SATA ports despite the size

## FAQ

### Will my DDR5-7200 kit run at 7200?

Osprey CPUs officially support 6400 MT/s, so the Lumen Apex DDR5-7200 runs at 6400 MT/s by default.
The board can run its 7200 MT/s profile when you enable XMP, which is a memory overclock.

### How many NVMe drives can I install?

Five, one of them at PCIe 5.0.

### Does it fit a mid tower?

Yes. It is a standard ATX board and fits any case that lists ATX support, such as the Tessel
Atlas Mid Tower or the Lattice Flow 500.
