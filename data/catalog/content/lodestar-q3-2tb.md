## Overview

The Lodestar Q3 2 TB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 3.0, reading at up to 3300 MB/s and writing at
up to 2800 MB/s. It uses QLC flash, which stores four bits per cell and makes large capacities
cheap. The trade-off is that after its fast cache fills, during very long writes, the write speed
drops well below the rated figure.

For a game library that is written once and read often, that hardly matters: load times are
close to any other NVMe drive.

## Who it's for

Gamers who want a large, cheap NVMe drive for their game library, as a second drive or a boot
drive.

## Pros

- 2 TB at a low price per terabyte
- NVMe load times for games
- Fits every M-key M.2 slot

## Cons

- QLC flash: slower sustained writes after the cache fills
- Lower endurance than TLC drives for heavy write workloads
- PCIe 3.0 speed

## FAQ

### Is QLC bad for games?

No. Games are mostly read, and reads stay fast. QLC slows down during very long writes, such as
copying hundreds of gigabytes at once.

### Is it good for video editing?

As a scratch disk for large recordings, a TLC drive such as the Arcwave A4 2 TB holds its write
speed much better.

### Will it work in my PCIe 4.0 slot?

Yes, at its own PCIe 3.0 speed.
