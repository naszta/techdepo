## Overview

The Meridian X870E Crest is a flagship ATX motherboard for AM5. It has four DDR5 slots (up to
256 GB, rated to 8000 MT/s), a PCIe 5.0 x16 graphics slot and four M-key M.2 slots: two at PCIe 5.0
and two at PCIe 4.0, the last of which also accepts M.2 SATA drives. Add four SATA ports, onboard
Wi-Fi and two 8-pin EPS headers for stable power to the hungriest CPUs.

It ships with BIOS 30 and supports Quartz and Obsidian CPUs out of the box, with BIOS flashback
for future upgrades.

## Who it's for

Enthusiasts and workstation builders pairing a Voltaris V9 8950 with fast PCIe 5.0 storage, lots of
memory and a high-end graphics card.

## Pros

- Two PCIe 5.0 M.2 slots plus two PCIe 4.0
- PCIe 5.0 graphics slot
- Up to 256 GB of DDR5, rated to 8000 MT/s
- Dual 8-pin EPS, Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback

## Cons

- Expensive
- With a CPU above 200 W, wants a power supply with two EPS cables
- DDR5 only

## FAQ

### Will DDR5-7200 memory run at full speed?

The board is rated to 8000 MT/s, but Obsidian CPUs officially support 5600 MT/s. A kit such as the
Lumen Apex DDR5-7200 runs at 5600 MT/s by default, and its 7200 MT/s profile is an overclock you
enable in the BIOS.

### Do I need to connect both EPS cables?

One 8-pin EPS cable is enough to boot and run, and enough for CPUs such as the Voltaris V5 8600
and V7 8700. A CPU that draws more than 200 W, such as the Voltaris V9 8950 (about 230 W), can be
held back under sustained load without the second, so the PC builder warns when the power supply
has only one. All our power supplies from 750 W up provide two.

### How many NVMe drives can I install?

Four: two at PCIe 5.0 and two at PCIe 4.0.
