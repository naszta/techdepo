## Overview

The Ferro Core 550 is a 550 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Bronze efficiency and fixed cables. It
has one 8-pin EPS connector for the CPU, two 8-pin PCIe connectors for the graphics card and five
SATA power connectors. It has no 12V-2x6 connector. It is 140 mm long.

550 W covers office PCs and entry-level gaming builds comfortably, such as a 65 W CPU with a card
like the Halcyon HX 6060 Dual. It is too small for high-power CPUs combined with 260 W-class or
bigger graphics cards.

## Who it's for

Office PCs and budget gaming builds with a 65 W-class CPU and a graphics card up to about 200 W.

## Pros

- Low price
- Enough for efficient CPUs with entry-level and mainstream cards
- Two PCIe 8-pin connectors

## Cons

- Too small for high-end builds
- No 12V-2x6 connector
- Non-modular cables and Bronze efficiency

## FAQ

### Can it power a Helion H7 1470 with an Aurex Nova 70?

No. The CPU can draw about 253 W and the card 263 W, which with the rest of the system exceeds
550 W. The PC builder flags it as an error. Choose 750 W or more, such as the Ferro Core 750.

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7070 Twin or SFF?

No. The HX 7070 needs a 12V-2x6 connector, which this unit does not have. The Nordwatt Prime 650 is
the closest alternative.

### Is 550 W enough for a Halcyon HX 6060 Dual?

Yes. The card recommends 550 W, and with a 65 W-class CPU there is plenty of headroom.
