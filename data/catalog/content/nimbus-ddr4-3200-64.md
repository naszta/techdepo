## Overview

The Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 64 GB kit has four 16 GB modules at 3200 MT/s and CL16, the official
memory speed of AM4 and of LGA1700 CPUs on DDR4 boards. It fills all four slots of a standard ATX
or Micro-ATX DDR4 board for a large-memory workstation at DDR4 prices.

The kit needs a board with four memory slots. Two-slot boards such as the Meridian A520M Core
cannot take it. Four modules mean two per memory channel, and CPUs officially support a lower
speed that way: 2667 MT/s on Granite (AM4) CPUs and 2933 MT/s on LGA1700 CPUs. The kit runs at that
speed by default; its 3200 MT/s profile usually works but is not guaranteed.

## Who it's for

DDR4 workstation builders who need 64 GB for virtual machines, large projects or photo and video
work, on boards like the Kestrel B550 Glide or Meridian B760M Forge D4.

## Pros

- 64 GB for large projects and virtual machines
- Low price per gigabyte for a matched four-module set
- Matched set of four modules

## Cons

- Needs a board with four memory slots
- Leaves no free slots for later upgrades
- Runs at 2667 MT/s (AM4) or 2933 MT/s (LGA1700) by default with four modules
- DDR4 only; does not fit DDR5 boards

## FAQ

### Will it work in the Meridian A520M Core?

No. That board has two memory slots, and this kit has four modules. For a two-slot AM4 board,
choose the Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 32 GB (2x16 GB).

### Can I combine it with another kit?

Our four-slot DDR4 boards are already full with this kit. Mixing kits is never guaranteed to
work, so buy the total capacity you need as one kit.

### Is 64 GB useful for gaming?

Not really; 32 GB is plenty for games. 64 GB helps with VMs, large codebases and media work.
