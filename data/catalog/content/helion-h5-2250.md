## Overview

The Helion H5 2250 is a fourteen-core LGA1851 processor from the Osprey generation, with six
performance and eight efficiency cores and one thread per core. It boosts to 5.2 GHz, has
integrated graphics and supports DDR5 officially up to 6400 MT/s, the highest of any CPU we sell.

Its rated TDP is 125 W and it draws up to about 159 W under sustained load, which a good
single-tower cooler handles. There is no cooler in the box. LGA1851 is a separate socket from
LGA1700: it needs an LGA1851 board such as the Kestrel B860I Hover or the Meridian Z890 Crest, and
DDR5 memory.

## Who it's for

Gamers and creators building a new Intel-socket system who want fast DDR5 support, integrated
graphics and good efficiency.

## Pros

- Fourteen cores with strong efficiency
- Official DDR5-6400 support
- Integrated graphics as a fallback display output

## Cons

- No cooler in the box
- DDR5 only
- No Hyper-Threading, so fewer threads than the core count suggests

## FAQ

### Does it fit LGA1700 boards?

No. LGA1851 is a different socket. Use an LGA1851 board such as the Kestrel B860I Hover or the
Meridian Z890 Crest.

### Which cooler should I buy?

A cooler rated for at least 159 W, its sustained draw, with an LGA1851 mount, such as the Breeze
Frost 120 (180 W) or, for small cases, the Breeze Glacier 120 (180 W). Low-profile air coolers are
rated too low. The Coldmark Classic 92 has no Intel mount.

### Which memory is best?

A two-module DDR5 kit at 6000 or 6400 MT/s, such as the Lumen Pulse DDR5-6400 64 GB or the Nimbus
Stratus DDR5-6000 32 GB.
