## Overview

The Kestrel B550 Glide is an ATX motherboard for AM4 processors with four DDR4 slots (up to
128 GB), two M.2 slots (one PCIe 4.0, one PCIe 3.0 that also takes M.2 SATA drives) and six SATA
ports. It keeps things simple: no Wi-Fi and no BIOS flashback, but solid power delivery for
six- and eight-core chips.

## Who it's for

Budget builders pairing an AM4 processor with DDR4 memory, and anyone with several SATA drives to
carry over from an old PC.

## Pros

- Affordable entry to AM4
- Six SATA ports
- First M.2 slot runs at PCIe 4.0 and fits long 22110 drives

## Cons

- No Wi-Fi
- No BIOS flashback
- AM4 and DDR4 only: no upgrade path to newer CPUs

## FAQ

### Does it support the Voltaris V5 5600?

Yes, out of the box. The board ships with BIOS 20, and Granite CPUs need BIOS 1 or newer.

### Can I use DDR5 memory?

No. The board is DDR4 only.

### Does it have Wi-Fi?

No. Use Ethernet, or add a PCIe or USB Wi-Fi adapter.
