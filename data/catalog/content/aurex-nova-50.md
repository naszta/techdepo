## Overview

The Aurex Nova 50 is a 170 mm, two-slot graphics card with 6 GB of VRAM that draws just 70 W, all
of it through the PCIe slot. It has no power connector at all, so it works with any power supply,
including the basic units in older office PCs.

It handles multiple displays, video playback, hardware video encoding and light or older games at
1080p. It is not meant for demanding new games.

## Who it's for

Office PCs that need more display outputs, upgrades of pre-built PCs with no spare PCIe power
cables, and builds around CPUs without integrated graphics that do not need gaming power.

## Pros

- No PCIe power cable needed
- Short 170 mm card fits every case we sell
- Low power and quiet
- Gives CPUs without integrated graphics a display output

## Cons

- Only 6 GB of VRAM
- Entry-level gaming performance

## FAQ

### Does it need a power cable?

No. It draws all 70 W from the PCIe slot. Any power supply of 300 W or more is enough for a
typical system with it.

### Can I use it with the Voltaris V5 5600?

Yes. The V5 5600 has no integrated graphics, and this card gives it display outputs at the lowest
cost. For real gaming, the Halcyon HX 6060 Dual is a better match.

### Will it fit a small-form-factor case?

Yes. At 170 mm it fits every case we sell, including the Tessel Sliver ITX (285 mm limit).
