## Overview

The Lumen Pulse DDR4-3600 32 GB kit has two 16 GB modules with an XMP profile for 3600 MT/s at CL18,
topped by an RGB light bar. It is 48 mm tall.

Know what the 3600 means. AM4 CPUs and LGA1700 CPUs on DDR4 boards officially support 3200 MT/s,
so the kit runs at 3200 MT/s by default. Enabling XMP sets 3600 MT/s, which is a memory overclock:
most boards handle it, but it is not guaranteed. The board's rating matters too. The Kestrel B550
Glide (4400 MT/s) and Meridian B760M Forge D4 (4000 MT/s) can run the profile, while the Meridian
A520M Core is rated to 3200 MT/s.

## Who it's for

DDR4 gamers who like RGB and want to tune their memory a little past the official speed.

## Pros

- 32 GB in two modules
- 3600 MT/s XMP profile for a small performance gain
- RGB light bar

## Cons

- Runs at 3200 MT/s unless you enable XMP
- 48 mm tall, so check clearance under large tower coolers
- DDR4 only; does not fit DDR5 boards

## FAQ

### Why does my PC show 3200 MT/s?

That is the official speed of the CPU's memory controller. Enable the XMP profile in the BIOS to
run the kit at 3600 MT/s, on boards rated for that speed.

### Will it fit under my air cooler?

At 48 mm it is taller than most budget kits. Single-tower coolers such as the Breeze Frost 120
leave room for about 42 mm in the slot next to the CPU, so the nearest module may touch the fan.
Raise the fan slightly or choose the 42 mm Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 32 GB (2x16 GB).

### Does it work on AM5?

No. AM5 is DDR5 only.
