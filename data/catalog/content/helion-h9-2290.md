## Overview

The Helion H9 2290 is the twenty-four-core flagship of the Osprey (LGA1851) generation, with
eight performance and sixteen efficiency cores. It boosts to 5.7 GHz, has integrated graphics and
supports DDR5 up to 6400 MT/s. Its base TDP is 125 W, but sustained all-core loads draw up to
about 250 W.

Plan for a 280 mm or 360 mm AIO or a large dual-tower cooler, and a power supply with plenty of
headroom when you pair it with a high-end graphics card. There is no cooler in the box. It needs
an LGA1851 board and DDR5 memory.

## Who it's for

Creators, developers and engineers who need many cores on a current Intel-socket platform, and
enthusiasts building a no-compromise workstation.

## Pros

- Twenty-four cores for heavy parallel work
- Official DDR5-6400 support
- Integrated graphics, useful for workstations

## Cons

- Up to 250 W under sustained load
- No cooler in the box
- DDR5 only

## FAQ

### Which cooler should I buy?

Not one rated for its 125 W TDP: sustained all-core work draws about 250 W, so choose a cooler
rated for 250 W or more, such as the Breeze Frost 140 Duo, the Coldmark Hydro 280 or a 360 mm
AIO.

### What power supply do I need?

With a high-end card such as the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple, choose 1000 W. With the Halcyon HX 7090
Triple, choose the 1200 W Nordwatt Titan 1200. The PC builder estimates the load and warns when
headroom drops below 20 %.

### Does it fit LGA1700 boards?

No. It needs an LGA1851 board such as the Meridian Z890 Crest.
