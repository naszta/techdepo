## Overview

The Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6000 32 GB kit gives you 32 GB in two 16 GB modules at 6000 MT/s and CL30.
Its heat spreader is only 34 mm tall, so it clears large tower coolers. Platforms that officially
support less than 6000 MT/s run it at their supported speed unless the memory profile is enabled.

## Who it's for

Gamers and creators on AM5 or DDR5 Intel platforms who want a fast, sensible 32 GB kit.

## Pros

- 32 GB is the sweet spot for gaming and multitasking
- Fast 6000 MT/s with tight CL30 timings
- Low-profile heat spreader fits under big coolers

## Cons

- DDR5 only; will not fit DDR4 motherboards
- Runs at the platform's official speed without its memory profile

## FAQ

### Does it work in DDR4 motherboards?

No. DDR5 and DDR4 modules are keyed differently and do not fit each other's slots.

### Will it run at 6000 MT/s?

With its memory profile enabled in the BIOS, on boards that support that speed. Otherwise it runs
at the CPU's officially supported speed, for example 5600 MT/s.

### Can I add a second kit later?

You can, on a four-slot board, but four modules run slower: with two per channel, CPUs officially
support 3600 MT/s (AM5 and LGA1700) or 4400 MT/s (LGA1851), and mixed kits are not guaranteed to
work together. Buying the capacity you need in one kit, such as the two-module Lumen Pulse
DDR5-6400 64 GB (2x32 GB), is more reliable.
