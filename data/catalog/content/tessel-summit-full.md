## Overview

The Tessel Summit Full Tower is our largest case. It takes E-ATX, ATX, Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX
boards, graphics cards up to 420 mm, air coolers up to 185 mm and ATX power supplies up to 250 mm.
The front and top each take radiators up to 420 mm, and the rear 120 or 140 mm. It has four
2.5-inch and six 3.5-inch bays, and four fans are included.

Every graphics card, air cooler, AIO and ATX power supply in our catalog fits, including the
Coldmark Hydro 420: it is the only case we sell with a 420 mm radiator mount. It has no SFX bracket,
so SFX power supplies do not fit.

## Who it's for

Workstation builders, storage hoarders and enthusiasts who want maximum clearance, airflow and
drive bays.

## FAQ

### Does the Coldmark Hydro 420 fit?

Yes, in the front or top. The Summit is the only case we sell with a 420 mm radiator mount.

### How many hard drives can I install?

Six 3.5-inch drives plus four 2.5-inch drives, limited in practice by your board's SATA ports and
your power supply's SATA connectors.

### Does the Nordwatt Titan 1200 fit?

Yes. It is 190 mm long and the Summit takes power supplies up to 250 mm.
