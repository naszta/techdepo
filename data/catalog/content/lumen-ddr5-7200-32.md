## Overview

The Lumen Apex DDR5-7200 32 GB kit has two 16 GB modules binned for 7200 MT/s at CL34, with a tall
52 mm heat spreader and RGB. It is for enthusiasts who want maximum memory bandwidth and are ready
to tune.

The speed depends on the rest of the build. No CPU we sell officially supports 7200 MT/s: Obsidian
(AM5) and Harrier (LGA1700) run at 5600 MT/s by default and Osprey (LGA1851) at 6400 MT/s. Enabling
the profile is a memory overclock, and the board must be rated for it. The Kestrel B650M Wing
(7200), Meridian X870E Crest (8000), Meridian Z790 Crest (7800) and Meridian Z890 Crest (8800) are.
The Meridian A620M Lite is rated to 6000 MT/s, so the kit never reaches 7200 there.

## Who it's for

Overclockers and benchmark enthusiasts with a high-end board.

## Pros

- Binned for 7200 MT/s
- Good fit for LGA1851 and high-end boards
- RGB light bar

## Cons

- Runs at the CPU's official speed (5600 or 6400 MT/s) by default
- 52 mm tall: may collide with tower cooler fans
- Little gain over DDR5-6000 in most games

## FAQ

### Will it run at 7200 MT/s on my V5 8600?

Not by default: it runs at 5600 MT/s, the CPU's official speed. With the profile enabled on a board
rated for 7200 MT/s it may reach 7200, but that is an overclock and not guaranteed.

### Is it worth it over DDR5-6000?

For gaming, rarely. For memory-bound work on LGA1851 or overclocking, it can be.

### Will it clear my cooler?

At 52 mm, it is best paired with an AIO or a cooler with offset fans. Single-tower coolers leave
about 42 mm.
