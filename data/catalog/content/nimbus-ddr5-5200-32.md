## Overview

The Nimbus Stratus DDR5-5200 32 GB kit has two 16 GB modules at 5200 MT/s and CL42, with a 32 mm
low-profile heat spreader that fits under any cooler. It costs about the same as a 32 GB DDR4 kit,
so it is the budget way to keep 32 GB when you move to an AM5, LGA1851 or DDR5 LGA1700 board.

5200 MT/s is the official memory speed of Quartz (AM5) CPUs, and Obsidian, Harrier and Osprey CPUs
support more, so on all of them it runs at its rated speed. On Falcon CPUs (4800 MT/s) and on the
Meridian H610M Base (4800 MT/s) it runs at 4800 MT/s.

## Who it's for

Budget gaming and productivity builds on DDR5 that want 32 GB for the price of DDR4.

## Pros

- 32 GB of DDR5 at a DDR4-like price
- Runs at its rated speed on most DDR5 CPUs without a memory profile
- Low-profile 32 mm modules clear every cooler

## Cons

- Slower than the 6000 MT/s kits that suit Obsidian CPUs best
- Loose CL42 timings
- DDR5 only; does not fit DDR4 boards

## FAQ

### I have a 32 GB DDR4 kit. Is this the DDR5 replacement?

Yes. DDR4 modules do not fit DDR5 boards such as the Kestrel B650M Wing, and this kit is the
32 GB DDR5 kit closest in price to the Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 32 GB (2x16 GB).

### Is it fast enough for gaming?

Yes. Faster kits such as the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6000 32 GB (2x16 GB) add a few percent in some
games, but 32 GB at 5200 MT/s is a solid base for gaming and multitasking.

### Will it work in a DDR4 board?

No. DDR5 modules are keyed differently and do not fit DDR4 slots, including the Meridian B760M
Forge D4 and all AM4 boards.
