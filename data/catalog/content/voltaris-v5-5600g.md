## Overview

The Voltaris V5 5600G is a six-core, twelve-thread AM4 processor from the Granite generation with
integrated graphics. It boosts to 4.4 GHz, runs DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s and ships with a stock
cooler, so CPU, board, memory and a drive are all you need for a working PC with a picture on
screen.

The integrated graphics handle office work, 4K video playback and older or lighter games at
1080p with reduced settings. Add a graphics card later and the CPU is still a capable gaming
chip, although the V5 5600 (without graphics) costs a little less if you know you will buy a
card from day one.

## Who it's for

Office, home-theatre and first-time builders who want a complete AM4 system without a graphics
card, and anyone who wants a fallback display output for troubleshooting.

## Pros

- Integrated graphics: no graphics card required
- Stock cooler included
- Runs on affordable AM4 boards and DDR4 memory
- Low 65 W TDP keeps the system quiet

## Cons

- Integrated graphics are not enough for demanding new games
- AM4 is the end of its upgrade line
- DDR4 only

## FAQ

### Can I use it without a graphics card?

Yes. The integrated graphics drive the motherboard's display outputs, so a monitor plugged into
the board works out of the box.

### What is the difference to the V5 5600?

The 5600G adds integrated graphics and a slightly higher base clock. The V5 5600 has no graphics
and needs a graphics card, but costs a little less.

### Which memory should I buy?

A dual-channel DDR4 kit at 3200 MT/s, such as the Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 16 GB or 32 GB. Two
modules matter more than usual here, because the integrated graphics share memory bandwidth.
