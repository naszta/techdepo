## Overview

The Lattice Core 200 is an affordable ATX mid tower with a mesh front and one fan included. It
takes ATX, Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX boards, graphics cards up to 315 mm, air coolers up to 158 mm and
ATX power supplies up to 170 mm. Radiators go in the front (120, 140 or 240 mm), top (120 or 240 mm)
or rear (120 mm). It has two 2.5-inch and two 3.5-inch bays.

The clearances suit mainstream builds. The longest high-end cards, 280 mm and larger radiators,
and the tallest dual-tower coolers do not fit.

## Who it's for

Budget builders putting together a mainstream gaming or office PC who want good airflow for little
money.

## Pros

- Very low price
- Mesh front for good airflow
- Takes every board size up to ATX
- Room for a 240 mm AIO in the front or top

## Cons

- Only one fan included; add more for a hot graphics card
- Graphics cards limited to 315 mm
- No 280 mm or 360 mm radiator support

## FAQ

### Does the Aurex Nova 80 fit?

No. The Nova 80 is 322 mm long and the Core 200 takes cards up to 315 mm. Cards up to the Halcyon
HX 7070 Twin (301 mm) fit; for longer cards, choose the Lattice Air 400 or Tessel Atlas Mid Tower.

### Which air coolers fit?

Coolers up to 158 mm tall, including the Breeze Frost 120 (155 mm) and Breeze Frost 140 Duo
(158 mm). The Coldmark Peak Duo (168 mm) is too tall.

### Can I install a 360 mm AIO?

No. The largest radiator is 240 mm, in the front or top.
