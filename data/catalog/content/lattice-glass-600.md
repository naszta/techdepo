## Overview

The Lattice Glass 600 is a dual-chamber showcase case with glass front and side panels. The power
supply and drives sit in a rear chamber, leaving the main chamber clear to show off the graphics
card and cooling. It takes ATX, Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX boards, graphics cards up to 410 mm, air
coolers up to 167 mm and ATX power supplies up to 220 mm.

Radiators fit in the side (up to 360 mm), top (up to 360 mm, including 280 mm) and bottom (up to
360 mm). It has four 2.5-inch and two 3.5-inch bays. No fans are included, so budget for them.

## Who it's for

Builders who want a striking showcase PC with RGB and liquid cooling.

## Pros

- Three 360 mm radiator mounts
- Fits graphics cards up to 410 mm
- Dual-chamber layout hides cables and the power supply
- Four 2.5-inch bays

## Cons

- No fans included
- Air coolers limited to 167 mm
- Glass panels show dust

## FAQ

### Are fans included?

No. Plan for at least three intake fans and one exhaust, or use AIO radiator fans as part of the
airflow.

### Does the Coldmark Peak Duo fit?

No, by 1 mm: it is 168 mm tall and the Glass 600 allows 167 mm. The Breeze Frost 140 Duo (158 mm)
fits, or use an AIO.

### Can I install two radiators?

Yes. The side, top and bottom mounts each take up to 360 mm, so a CPU AIO and extra radiators can
share the case.
