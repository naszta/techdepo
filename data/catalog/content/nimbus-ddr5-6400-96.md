## Overview

The Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6400 96 GB kit has two 48 GB modules rated for 6400 MT/s at CL32, with
low 34 mm heat spreaders. 48 GB modules give 96 GB in two slots, so even Mini-ITX boards can carry
workstation amounts of memory.

Check the board's maximum. The Kestrel B650E-I Perch (96 GB), Kestrel B860I Hover (128 GB) and all
four-slot DDR5 boards we sell take it. The Meridian A620M Lite supports only 64 GB, so the kit is
too large for it. It runs at 6400 MT/s on Osprey (LGA1851) CPUs. By default it runs at 5600 MT/s on
Obsidian (AM5) and Harrier (LGA1700) CPUs, 5200 MT/s on Quartz (AM5) and 4800 MT/s on Falcon
(LGA1700).

## Who it's for

Developers, data scientists and video editors who need lots of memory for VMs, datasets or 8K
timelines.

## Pros

- 96 GB in two modules
- Fits Mini-ITX boards with 96 GB support or more
- Low-profile modules clear any cooler

## Cons

- Too large for boards with a 64 GB maximum
- Runs below its rated speed on AM5 and LGA1700 CPUs by default (5600 MT/s or lower)
- Expensive

## FAQ

### Will it work with the Meridian A620M Lite?

No. That board supports up to 64 GB. Choose the Lumen Pulse DDR5-6400 64 GB (2x32 GB) instead.

### Can I add a second kit later?

On a four-slot board, two kits make four modules and 192 GB. With two modules per channel, CPUs
officially support only 3600 MT/s (AM5 and LGA1700) or 4400 MT/s (LGA1851), and mixing kits is not
guaranteed to work, so buy one kit with the total you need where possible.

### Is it overkill for gaming?

Yes. Games do not use more than 32 GB.
