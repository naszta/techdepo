## Overview

The Arcwave A3 500 GB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 3.0, reading at up to 3500 MB/s and writing at
up to 2300 MB/s. That is several times faster than any SATA drive, and more than enough for a
snappy operating system, office apps and everyday use.

It works in any M-key M.2 slot: PCIe 4.0 and 5.0 slots are backwards compatible, so it simply runs
at its own PCIe 3.0 speed there.

## Who it's for

Office PCs, home servers and starter builds that need a fast, inexpensive boot drive.

## Pros

- Very low price
- Fits every M-key M.2 slot on every board we sell
- Far faster than SATA SSDs and hard drives

## Cons

- 500 GB fills quickly with modern games
- PCIe 3.0 speed, behind Gen 4 drives in large file copies

## FAQ

### Is 500 GB enough?

For the operating system and apps, yes. A few modern games can fill it, so gamers should consider
1 TB or a second drive.

### Will it work in a PCIe 4.0 slot?

Yes. It runs at its PCIe 3.0 speed in Gen 4 and Gen 5 slots.

### Can I put it in a slot marked SATA only?

None of our boards have SATA-only M.2 slots. It needs an M-key slot with PCIe (NVMe) support,
which every M-key slot we list provides.
