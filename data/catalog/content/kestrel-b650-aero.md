## Overview

The Kestrel B650 Aero is a full-size ATX motherboard for AM5. It has four DDR5 slots (up to
192 GB, rated to 7200 MT/s), three M-key M.2 slots at PCIe 4.0 (the third also accepts M.2 SATA
drives), four SATA ports, onboard Wi-Fi and a PCIe 4.0 x16 graphics slot.

It ships with BIOS 22, which already supports both AM5 families: Quartz and Obsidian CPUs boot
without an update. BIOS flashback is there anyway for future CPUs. An 8-pin plus a 4-pin EPS
header give the CPU plenty of power for anything up to the Voltaris V9 8950.

## Who it's for

Gamers and creators building a mid-range AM5 PC in an ATX case who want room for several SSDs
and no BIOS hassle.

## Pros

- Supports every AM5 CPU out of the box
- Three PCIe 4.0 M.2 slots
- Onboard Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback
- Four DDR5 slots up to 192 GB

## Cons

- DDR5 only; DDR4 kits do not fit
- PCIe 4.0 graphics and M.2, not 5.0
- ATX size needs a mid tower or larger

## FAQ

### Will the Kestrel B650 Aero work with my Nimbus DDR4 kit?

No. Like every AM5 board, it takes DDR5 only, and DDR4 modules are keyed differently and do not
fit. Pair it with a DDR5 kit: the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-4800 16 GB (2x8 GB) and Nimbus Stratus
DDR5-5200 32 GB (2x16 GB) cost about the same as 16 GB and 32 GB DDR4 kits.

### Does it need a BIOS update for the Voltaris V5 8600?

No. It ships with BIOS 22, and Obsidian CPUs need BIOS 16.

### Will it fit a Micro-ATX case?

No. It is an ATX board. Micro-ATX-only cases such as the Lattice M1 Mini and Tessel Cube M need
the Kestrel B650M Wing instead.
