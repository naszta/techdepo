## Overview

The Kestrel B650E-I Perch is a Mini-ITX motherboard for AM5 built for compact high-end PCs. It has
two DDR5 slots (up to 96 GB, rated to 7600 MT/s), a PCIe 5.0 x16 graphics slot, two M-key M.2 slots
(one PCIe 5.0, one PCIe 4.0, both NVMe only), two SATA ports and onboard Wi-Fi.

It ships with BIOS 24, so Quartz and Obsidian CPUs both work out of the box, and BIOS flashback
is included. The single 8-pin EPS header handles every AM5 CPU, but in a small case the cooler is
usually the real limit for a 170 W chip.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders who want a powerful AM5 system in cases like the Tessel Nook ITX,
Tessel Sliver ITX or Lattice Pocket ITX.

## Pros

- PCIe 5.0 for graphics and the primary M.2 slot
- Supports every AM5 CPU out of the box
- Onboard Wi-Fi and BIOS flashback
- Fits every case size

## Cons

- Two memory slots, 96 GB maximum
- Only two SATA ports
- M.2 slots do not accept M.2 SATA drives
- Expensive, as small boards tend to be

## FAQ

### Can I use a 4x16 GB memory kit?

No. The board has two slots, so a four-module kit cannot be installed. For 64 GB, choose the Lumen
Pulse DDR5-6400 64 GB (2x32 GB); for 96 GB, the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-6400 96 GB (2x48 GB).

### How many drives can I install?

Two NVMe SSDs in the M.2 slots and two SATA drives on the SATA ports, if your case has the bays.
Three SATA drives are one too many.

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

Yes. It is a Mini-ITX board, which every case we sell accepts.
