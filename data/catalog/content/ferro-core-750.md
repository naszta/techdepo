## Overview

The Ferro Core 750 is a 750 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Bronze efficiency and semi-modular
cables: the main and CPU cables are fixed, and the PCIe and SATA cables detach. It provides two
8-pin EPS, four 8-pin PCIe and six SATA power connectors. It has no 12V-2x6 connector. It is 150 mm
long.

Four PCIe 8-pin connectors make it the most affordable unit in our range for cards that need three
of them, such as the Aurex Nova 80 and Nova 90.

## Who it's for

Gamers with a high-power card on classic 8-pin power who want enough wattage without paying for
Gold efficiency.

## Pros

- Four PCIe 8-pin connectors
- Two EPS connectors for high-end boards
- Semi-modular cables
- Great value per watt

## Cons

- No 12V-2x6 connector
- Bronze efficiency means a bit more heat and noise under load

## FAQ

### Can it power the Aurex Nova 80?

Yes. The Nova 80 needs three 8-pin PCIe connectors and this unit has four. With a 65 W-class CPU,
750 W is enough; with a high-power CPU, choose 1000 W.

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple?

No. That card needs a 12V-2x6 connector, which this unit does not have. Choose a unit with a native
12V-2x6 cable instead, such as the Nordwatt Prime 750.

### Does it fit the Lattice M1 Mini?

Yes. It is 150 mm long and the M1 Mini takes ATX units up to 160 mm.
