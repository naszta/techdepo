## Overview

The Tessel Nook is a compact Mini-ITX case for living-room and desk builds. It takes SFX power
supplies up to 130 mm, graphics cards up to 300 mm and air coolers up to 70 mm tall. For hotter
CPUs, the side mount takes a 120 mm or 240 mm AIO radiator. It has two 2.5" bays and no 3.5" bays.

## Who it's for

Builders who want a small, tidy PC and are happy to plan around tight clearances.

## Pros

- Very small footprint
- Fits a 240 mm AIO
- Takes most mid-length graphics cards

## Cons

- Mini-ITX boards and SFX power supplies only
- Air coolers limited to 70 mm, so most tower coolers do not fit
- No room for 3.5" drives

## FAQ

### Will a tower air cooler fit?

Probably not: the limit is 70 mm. Use a low-profile cooler or a 240 mm AIO on the side mount.

### Can I use an ATX power supply?

No. It only takes SFX units up to 130 mm, such as the Ferro SF750.

### Does the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple fit?

No. It is 336 mm long, and the Nook takes cards up to 300 mm.

### Does the Halcyon HX 7070 Twin fit?

No, by 1 mm: the Twin is 301 mm long. The Halcyon HX 7070 SFF has the same graphics chip in a
248 mm body and fits.
