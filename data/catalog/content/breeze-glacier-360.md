## Overview

The Breeze Glacier 360 is an all-in-one liquid cooler with a 360 mm radiator and three RGB fans,
rated for 300 W. It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. It keeps the hottest CPUs we
sell cool under sustained load.

It needs a 360 mm radiator mount: the Lattice Air 400 (front), Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (front),
Tessel Cube M (top), Lattice Flow 500, Lattice Glass 600 and Tessel Summit Full Tower have one. The
Tessel Nook ITX (240 mm maximum), Lattice Core 200, Lattice M1 Mini, Lattice Pocket ITX and Tessel
Sliver ITX do not.

## Who it's for

Builders with high-power CPUs who want strong cooling and RGB in a mid tower or larger.

## Pros

- Rated for 300 W
- Three RGB fans
- Mounts for every current socket

## Cons

- Needs a 360 mm radiator mount
- Pump adds a faint hum
- RGB cables add clutter

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

No. The Nook's side mount takes 120 mm or 240 mm radiators only. The Breeze Glacier 240 fits.

### Does it fit the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower?

Yes, in the front mount. The top mount takes up to 280 mm.

### Is it better than the Coldmark Hydro 360?

The Glacier has RGB fans; the Hydro has a thicker radiator, a higher 320 W rating and runs quieter.
