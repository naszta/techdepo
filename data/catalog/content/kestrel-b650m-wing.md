## Overview

The Kestrel B650M Wing is a Micro-ATX AM5 motherboard with four DDR5 slots (up to 192 GB), two
M-key M.2 slots at PCIe 4.0, four SATA ports and onboard Wi-Fi. A PCIe 4.0 x16 slot takes any
current graphics card. BIOS flashback lets you update the firmware from a USB stick without a CPU
installed.

## Who it's for

Builders who want a compact, well-connected AM5 board for a gaming or work PC without paying for
overclocking features they will not use.

## Pros

- Two PCIe 4.0 M.2 slots for fast NVMe drives
- Onboard Wi-Fi
- BIOS flashback for painless CPU upgrades
- Fits Micro-ATX and larger cases

## Cons

- Ships with a BIOS that predates second-generation AM5 (Obsidian) CPUs
- DDR5 only
- Only four SATA ports

## FAQ

### Does it work with the Voltaris V5 8600?

Yes, after a BIOS update: the board ships with BIOS 10 and Obsidian CPUs need BIOS 16. Thanks to
BIOS flashback you can update it from a USB stick before installing the CPU.

### Will the Kestrel B650M Wing work with my Nimbus DDR4 kit?

No. The slots are DDR5 only; DDR4 modules such as the Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 16 GB (2x8 GB) are
keyed differently and physically do not fit. The DDR5 kits closest in price are the Nimbus Stratus
DDR5-4800 16 GB (2x8 GB) and the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-5200 32 GB (2x16 GB).

### How many NVMe drives can I install?

Two, in the two M-key slots. The third M.2 slot is E-key and only takes a Wi-Fi module.
