## Overview

The Helion H5 1440F is a ten-core, sixteen-thread LGA1700 processor from the Harrier generation:
six performance cores with Hyper-Threading plus four efficiency cores. It boosts to 4.7 GHz and
comes with a stock cooler. The F means no integrated graphics, so a graphics card is required.

Its rated TDP is 65 W, but it can draw up to about 148 W in short bursts and sustained heavy
loads. The stock cooler is fine for gaming; for long rendering or encoding sessions a tower
cooler keeps it faster and quieter. It runs DDR5 up to 5600 MT/s or DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s,
depending on the board. Boards from the first LGA1700 generation need a Harrier-capable BIOS.

## Who it's for

Gamers building a mid-range LGA1700 PC who will buy a graphics card anyway, and DDR4 owners who
want a modern CPU without buying new memory.

## Pros

- Ten cores for gaming and multitasking
- Stock cooler included
- Works with DDR4 or DDR5 boards

## Cons

- No integrated graphics; a graphics card is mandatory
- Peak power is far above the 65 W TDP under heavy load
- LGA1700 has no further CPU generations to come

## FAQ

### Can I use it without a graphics card?

No. The F model has no integrated graphics, so nothing appears on screen without a graphics card.
If you want a display fallback, choose the Helion H3 1210 or H7 1470.

### Is the stock cooler good enough?

For gaming and everyday use, yes. For sustained all-core loads, a tower cooler such as the Breeze
Frost 120 keeps clocks higher and noise lower.

### Which boards support it?

The Meridian B760M Forge D4 (DDR4), the Kestrel B760 Vector and the Meridian Z790 Crest (both
DDR5). They ship with a BIOS that already supports Harrier. The Meridian H610M Base does not
support it with any BIOS version, because it runs Falcon CPUs only.
