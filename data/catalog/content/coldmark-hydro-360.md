## Overview

The Coldmark Hydro 360 is an all-in-one liquid cooler with a thick 360 mm radiator and three quiet
120 mm fans, rated for 320 W. It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. The thicker
radiator lets the fans spin slower for the same cooling, so it stays quiet even with a Helion H7
1470 or Helion H9 2290 at full load.

It needs a 360 mm radiator mount, which the Lattice Air 400, Tessel Atlas Mid Tower, Tessel Cube M,
Lattice Flow 500, Lattice Glass 600 and Tessel Summit Full Tower have.

## Who it's for

Workstation builders and enthusiasts who want the quietest cooling for a high-power CPU.

## Pros

- Rated for 320 W
- Thick radiator for low fan speeds
- Mounts for every current socket

## Cons

- Needs a 360 mm radiator mount
- Thick radiator adds weight
- No RGB

## FAQ

### Which cases fit it?

Any case with a 360 mm mount: the Lattice Air 400, Tessel Atlas Mid Tower, Tessel Cube M, Lattice
Flow 500, Lattice Glass 600 and Tessel Summit Full Tower.

### Is it overkill for a Voltaris V5 8600?

Yes. A 65 W CPU is well served by a Breeze Frost 120.

### Front or top mount?

Front gives the CPU cooler air; top keeps the graphics card cooler. Both work.
