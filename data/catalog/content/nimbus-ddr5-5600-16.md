## Overview

The Nimbus Stratus DDR5-5600 16 GB kit has two 8 GB modules at 5600 MT/s and CL46, with a 32 mm
low-profile heat spreader that fits under any cooler. 5600 MT/s is the official memory speed of
Obsidian (AM5) and Harrier (LGA1700) CPUs, so on those it runs at its rated speed. On Quartz CPUs,
which officially support 5200 MT/s, and Falcon CPUs (4800 MT/s), it runs at the CPU's speed.

## Who it's for

Office PCs, home servers and budget DDR5 builds where 16 GB is enough.

## Pros

- Low price for DDR5
- Low-profile 32 mm modules clear every cooler
- Runs at rated speed on Obsidian and Harrier CPUs

## Cons

- 16 GB is the minimum for gaming today
- Loose CL46 timings
- DDR5 only; does not fit DDR4 boards

## FAQ

### Is 16 GB enough?

For office work, browsing and lighter games, yes. For modern games with a browser open, or for
creative work, choose 32 GB, such as the Nimbus Stratus DDR5-5600 32 GB.

### Will it work in a DDR4 board?

No. DDR5 modules are keyed differently and do not fit DDR4 slots, including the Meridian B760M
Forge D4 and all AM4 boards.

### Why does it run at 5200 MT/s on my V5 7600?

Quartz CPUs officially support 5200 MT/s. Enabling the kit's memory profile sets 5600 MT/s.
