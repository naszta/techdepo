## Overview

The Breeze Glacier 240 is an all-in-one liquid cooler with a 240 mm radiator and two 120 mm fans,
rated for 250 W. Because the pump block is low, it is a good way to cool a hot CPU in a case with
little vertical clearance, as long as the case has a 240 mm radiator mount. It supports AM4, AM5,
LGA1700 and LGA1851.

## Who it's for

Builders with high-end CPUs, or with compact cases that take a 240 mm radiator but not a tall air
cooler.

## Pros

- Handles hot CPUs comfortably
- Low block height avoids tall-cooler limits
- Mounts for every current socket

## Cons

- Needs a 240 mm radiator mount in the case
- Pump adds a faint hum and a potential point of failure

## FAQ

### Will it fit my case?

Your case needs a position that takes a 240 mm radiator, for example the front or top of most mid
towers, or the side of the Tessel Nook ITX.

### Is it better than an air cooler?

For CPUs above about 150 W, usually yes. For mainstream CPUs, a good air cooler is quieter and
cheaper.

### Does it need maintenance?

No. It is a sealed loop and needs no refilling.
