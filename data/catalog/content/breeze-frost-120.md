## Overview

The Breeze Frost 120 is a single-tower air cooler with a 120 mm fan, rated for 180 W. It is 155 mm
tall and includes mounting hardware for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. It stays quiet with
mainstream CPUs and is much better than most stock coolers.

## Who it's for

Builders who want a quiet, reliable cooler for a mid-range CPU in a standard tower case.

## Pros

- Quiet under typical loads
- Mounts for every current socket included
- Great value

## Cons

- 155 mm tall: does not fit compact cases
- Not enough for the hottest high-end CPUs under sustained load

## FAQ

### Does it fit my case?

Your case must allow coolers of at least 155 mm. Most mid towers do; small-form-factor cases
usually do not.

### Does it support AM5?

Yes. The mounting kit covers AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851.

### Does it block memory slots?

No. It leaves room for memory modules up to about 42 mm tall.
