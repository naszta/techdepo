## Overview

The Breeze Whisper LP is a low-profile air cooler only 47 mm tall, rated for 95 W. It includes
mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. It fits under the lowest clearances we sell, including
the Tessel Sliver ITX (58 mm) and Tessel Nook ITX (70 mm).

The rating is the limit. It handles CPUs that draw up to 95 W under sustained load, such as the
Voltaris V5 7600 and V5 8600 (about 88 W) and the Helion H3 1210 (about 89 W). Hotter CPUs, such
as the Voltaris V7 8700 (about 162 W) or the Helion H5 1440F, which has a 65 W TDP but draws up to
about 148 W, will run hot and throttle with it.

## Who it's for

Slim and small-form-factor builds with a 65 W-class CPU.

## Pros

- Only 47 mm tall: fits every case we sell
- Mounts for every current socket
- Clears tall memory modules

## Cons

- Rated for 95 W only
- Audible under sustained full load

## FAQ

### Will it cool a Voltaris V7 8700?

Not properly. The V7 8700 draws up to about 162 W under sustained load and this cooler is rated for
95 W, so the CPU will throttle. In a slim case, choose a 65 W CPU such as the V5 8600; in the Tessel
Nook ITX, an AIO such as the Breeze Glacier 240 (250 W) on the side mount cools it properly.

### Does it fit the Tessel Sliver ITX?

Yes. The Sliver allows 58 mm and the Whisper LP is 47 mm tall.

### Does it block memory slots?

No. Its low height and compact heatsink clear any memory module.
