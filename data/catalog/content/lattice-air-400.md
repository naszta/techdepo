## Overview

The Lattice Air 400 is a budget airflow mid tower with three fans included. It takes ATX,
Micro-ATX and Mini-ITX boards, graphics cards up to 330 mm, air coolers up to 162 mm and ATX power
supplies up to 180 mm. The front takes radiators up to 360 mm, the top up to 240 mm and the rear
120 mm. It has two 2.5-inch and two 3.5-inch bays.

It is the cheapest case in our range with a 360 mm radiator mount, which makes it a good home for
a hot CPU on a budget.

## Who it's for

Gamers who want strong airflow and room for a 360 mm AIO without paying mid-range prices.

## Pros

- Three fans included
- 360 mm radiator support in the front
- Takes every board size up to ATX
- Good value

## Cons

- Graphics cards limited to 330 mm
- Air coolers limited to 162 mm
- Power supplies limited to 180 mm

## FAQ

### Does the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple fit?

No. It is 336 mm long and the Air 400 takes cards up to 330 mm. The Aurex Nova 80 (322 mm) fits.
For the HX 7080, choose the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (360 mm) or Lattice Flow 500 (400 mm).

### Can I use the Breeze Glacier 360?

Yes, in the front mount.

### Does the Nordwatt Titan 1200 fit?

No. It is 190 mm long and the limit is 180 mm. The Nordwatt Prime 1000 (170 mm) fits.
