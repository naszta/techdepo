## Overview

The Lodestar Vault 4 TB is a 3.5-inch SATA hard drive reading and writing at up to 190 MB/s. It
offers the lowest price per terabyte in our range, for backups, photo archives, media and
anything that does not need SSD speed.

It needs a 3.5-inch bay, a free SATA port and a SATA power connector. Small-form-factor cases
such as the Tessel Nook ITX and Tessel Sliver ITX have no 3.5-inch bays, so it does not fit them.

## Who it's for

Builders who need a lot of affordable storage for backups and media alongside an SSD for the
system.

## Pros

- Lowest cost per terabyte
- 4 TB for backups and archives
- Standard SATA connection

## Cons

- Much slower than any SSD; not suitable as a boot drive
- Audible when seeking
- Needs a 3.5-inch bay, which small cases lack

## FAQ

### Can I install Windows on it?

You can, but it will feel slow. Use an SSD for the system and keep this drive for data.

### Which cases fit it?

Any case with a 3.5-inch bay: most of our towers have two, the Tessel Summit Full Tower has six,
and the Lattice M1 Mini, Lattice Pocket ITX and Tessel Cube M have one. The Tessel Nook ITX and
Tessel Sliver ITX have none.

### Is it good for games?

It works, but load times are long. Keep current games on an SSD.
