## Overview

The Ferro SF750 is a 750 W SFX power supply with 80 PLUS Gold efficiency and fully modular
cables. Despite its 100 mm depth it provides two 8-pin EPS, two 8-pin PCIe and one native 12V-2x6
connector, so it can power a high-end graphics card in a small-form-factor build.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders who need serious power in a tiny package.

## Pros

- Compact SFX size
- Native 12V-2x6 cable
- Fully modular for tidy cable management
- Gold efficiency

## Cons

- Costs more than an ATX unit of the same wattage
- Fan can be audible under full load

## FAQ

### Does it fit ATX cases?

With a bracket. It needs an SFX-to-ATX bracket, which is not included and which our ATX cases do not
ship with, so the PC builder shows a warning until you have one. The Lattice Pocket ITX includes a
bracket and takes it.

### Can it power a 320 W graphics card?

Usually, with a mid-range CPU. Check the PC builder's load estimate: it warns when headroom drops
below 20 %.

### Are the cables included?

Yes, every cable is included and detachable.
