## Overview

The Halcyon HX 7070 SFF is the HX 7070 graphics chip in a compact 248 mm, 2.5-slot body, made for
small-form-factor builds. It has 12 GB of VRAM, draws 250 W and takes power through a single 12V-2x6
(16-pin) connector; no adapter is included. The vendor recommends a 700 W power supply.

It fits every case we sell except the slim Tessel Sliver ITX, which takes two-slot cards only. In
the Tessel Nook ITX (300 mm), an SFX case, the Ferro SF750 has the native 12V-2x6 cable it needs;
the Ferro SF600 does not.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders who want strong 1440p and ray-tracing performance in a shoebox-sized
PC.

## Pros

- 248 mm long: fits every case we sell except the slim Tessel Sliver ITX
- Same performance as the HX 7070 Twin
- One 12V-2x6 cable keeps a small build tidy

## Cons

- Needs a PSU with a native 12V-2x6 cable
- Runs warmer and louder than the larger Twin version
- Costs a little more than the Twin
- 2.5 slots thick: too thick for the Tessel Sliver ITX

## FAQ

### Which SFX power supply should I use?

The Ferro SF750 (100 mm) and the Ferro SF850L (130 mm) both fit the Tessel Nook ITX and have a
native 12V-2x6 cable. The Ferro SF600 has no 12V-2x6 connector.

### Does it fit the Tessel Nook ITX?

Yes, with 52 mm to spare.

### Is it slower than the Twin?

Not meaningfully. It runs a few degrees warmer and its fans spin faster under load.
