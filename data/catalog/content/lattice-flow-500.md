## Overview

The Lattice Flow 500 is a roomy airflow mid tower for high-end builds. It takes ATX, Micro-ATX and
Mini-ITX boards, graphics cards up to 400 mm, air coolers up to 175 mm and ATX power supplies up to
220 mm. The front and top each take radiators up to 360 mm, and the rear 120 or 140 mm. It has two
2.5-inch bays, two 3.5-inch bays and three included fans.

Every graphics card, air cooler and ATX power supply we sell fits. It does not take a 420 mm
radiator, and like our other ATX cases it has no SFX bracket, so an SFX power supply needs an
SFX-to-ATX bracket bought separately.

## Who it's for

Gamers and creators building a powerful PC who want generous clearances and strong airflow without
a full tower.

## Pros

- Fits every graphics card, air cooler and ATX power supply we sell
- 360 mm radiators front and top
- Three fans included
- Takes power supplies up to 220 mm

## Cons

- No 420 mm radiator support
- Larger than a typical mid tower
- No E-ATX support

## FAQ

### Does the Halcyon HX 7090 Triple fit?

Yes. It is 357 mm long and the Flow 500 takes up to 400 mm.

### Can I install a 420 mm AIO?

No. The largest radiator is 360 mm. For the Coldmark Hydro 420, choose the Tessel Summit Full
Tower.

### Does the Coldmark Peak Duo fit?

Yes. It is 168 mm tall and the Flow 500 allows 175 mm.
