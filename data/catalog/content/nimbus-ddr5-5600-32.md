## Overview

The Nimbus Stratus DDR5-5600 32 GB kit has two 16 GB modules at 5600 MT/s and CL40, with a 32 mm
low-profile heat spreader. 5600 MT/s is the official memory speed of Obsidian (AM5) and Harrier
(LGA1700) CPUs, so it runs at rated speed on those platforms without any profile.

It fits every DDR5 board we sell, including two-slot boards, and leaves room under even the
largest air coolers.

## Who it's for

Budget and mid-range DDR5 gaming builds that want 32 GB without paying for faster bins.

## Pros

- 32 GB at a budget price
- Rated speed matches what Obsidian and Harrier CPUs officially support
- Low-profile 32 mm modules

## Cons

- Slower than DDR5-6000 kits in memory-sensitive games
- DDR5 only; does not fit DDR4 boards
- No RGB lighting

## FAQ

### Should I get DDR5-6000 instead?

On AM5, DDR5-6000 with tight timings gives a few percent more performance in some games, but it
is a memory overclock. This kit runs at its rated speed with no tuning.

### Does it work on LGA1851?

Yes. Osprey CPUs support up to 6400 MT/s, so this kit runs at its rated 5600 MT/s.

### Can I use it with my DDR4 board?

No. DDR5 does not fit DDR4 slots.
