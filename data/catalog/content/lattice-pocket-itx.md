## Overview

The Lattice Pocket ITX is a compact Mini-ITX tower that avoids the usual small-case compromises.
It takes a regular ATX power supply up to 160 mm, or an SFX unit using the included bracket, so you
do not have to pay for an SFX unit. It fits graphics cards up to 330 mm, air coolers up to 160 mm
and front radiators up to 280 mm. It has two 2.5-inch bays, one 3.5-inch bay and two fans
included.

## Who it's for

Builders who want a small Mini-ITX PC with high-end parts but prefer affordable ATX power supplies
and full-size coolers.

## Pros

- Takes ATX or SFX power supplies (SFX bracket included)
- Fits graphics cards up to 330 mm
- Room for a 160 mm tower cooler or a 280 mm AIO
- Two fans included

## Cons

- Mini-ITX boards only
- ATX power supplies limited to 160 mm
- No 360 mm radiator support

## FAQ

### Can I use an ATX power supply?

Yes, up to 160 mm long: the Ferro Core 550, 650 and 750 and the Nordwatt Prime 650, 750 and 850. The
Nordwatt Prime 1000 and Titan 1200 are too long. SFX units fit with the included bracket.

### Which motherboards fit?

Mini-ITX only, such as the Kestrel B650E-I Perch (AM5) or Kestrel B860I Hover (LGA1851).

### Does the Aurex Nova 80 fit?

Yes. It is 322 mm long and the Pocket takes up to 330 mm. Pair it with an ATX unit that has three
8-pin PCIe cables, such as the Ferro Core 750 or Nordwatt Prime 750.
