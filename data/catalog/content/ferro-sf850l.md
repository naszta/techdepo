## Overview

The Ferro SF850L is an 850 W SFX-L power supply with 80 PLUS Platinum efficiency and fully modular
cables. SFX-L units are 130 mm long instead of 100 mm, which leaves room for a quieter 120 mm fan.
It provides two 8-pin EPS, two 8-pin PCIe, one native 12V-2x6 (16-pin) and four SATA power
connectors.

Check the case's power supply length limit. It fits the Tessel Nook ITX (130 mm) and the Lattice
Pocket ITX (160 mm), but not the Tessel Sliver ITX, which takes 100 mm SFX units only.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders running a high-end card and CPU who want the quietest SFX-class unit.

## Pros

- 850 W in a small SFX-L package
- Native 12V-2x6 cable
- Platinum efficiency and a quiet 120 mm fan

## Cons

- 130 mm long: too long for 100 mm SFX cases
- Only two PCIe 8-pin connectors
- Expensive

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Tessel Sliver ITX?

No. The Sliver takes SFX units up to 100 mm and this one is 130 mm. The Ferro SF750 fits.

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7080 Triple?

Electrically yes: it has the 12V-2x6 cable and 850 W. But the HX 7080 is 336 mm long and does not
fit our SFX cases.

### Can it power the Aurex Nova 80?

No. That card needs three 8-pin PCIe connectors and this unit has two.
