## Overview

The Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 32 GB kit has two 16 GB modules at 3200 MT/s and CL16. 3200 MT/s is
the official memory speed of Granite (AM4) CPUs and of LGA1700 CPUs on DDR4 boards, so it runs at
full speed on those platforms without any tuning. The 42 mm heat spreader clears most tower
coolers.

## Who it's for

Gamers and multitaskers on AM4 or on DDR4 LGA1700 boards such as the Meridian B760M Forge D4 who
want the comfortable amount of memory for current games, browsers and creative apps.

## Pros

- 32 GB in two modules for full dual-channel bandwidth
- Runs at its rated speed on AM4 and DDR4 Intel boards
- Leaves two slots free on four-slot boards

## Cons

- DDR4 only; does not fit DDR5 boards (AM5, LGA1851, DDR5 LGA1700 boards)
- No RGB lighting

## FAQ

### Will it work with a Kestrel B650M Wing or another AM5 board?

No. AM5 boards such as the Kestrel B650M Wing, Kestrel B650 Aero and Kestrel B650E-I Perch use
DDR5 slots, and DDR4 modules do not fit them. The 32 GB DDR5 kit in the same price range is the
Nimbus Stratus DDR5-5200 32 GB (2x16 GB).

### Does it work in a two-slot board?

Yes. With two modules, it fits two-slot boards such as the Meridian A520M Core.

### Do I need to enable XMP?

Usually not on AM4 and LGA1700, whose CPUs officially support 3200 MT/s. If the BIOS shows a lower
speed, enabling the XMP profile sets 3200 MT/s.
