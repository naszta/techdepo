## Overview

The Nimbus Stratus DDR5-4800 16 GB kit has two 8 GB modules at 4800 MT/s and CL40, the base JEDEC
speed of DDR5. Every DDR5 CPU and board we sell supports 4800 MT/s officially, so the kit runs at
its rated speed everywhere, with no memory profile to enable. Its 32 mm low-profile heat spreader
fits under any cooler.

It costs about the same as a 16 GB DDR4 kit, which makes it the natural choice when you move a
budget build from DDR4 to an AM5, LGA1851 or DDR5 LGA1700 board.

## Who it's for

Office PCs, home servers and budget DDR5 builds where 16 GB is enough and price matters most.

## Pros

- The lowest price for DDR5 in our range
- Runs at its rated speed on every DDR5 platform
- Low-profile 32 mm modules clear every cooler

## Cons

- 16 GB is the minimum for gaming today
- 4800 MT/s is slower than the 5200 to 6400 MT/s current CPUs support
- DDR5 only; does not fit DDR4 boards

## FAQ

### I have a 16 GB DDR4 kit. Is this the DDR5 replacement?

Yes. DDR4 modules do not fit DDR5 boards such as the Kestrel B650M Wing, and this kit is the DDR5
kit closest in price to the Nimbus Cirrus DDR4-3200 16 GB (2x8 GB). For more speed, the Nimbus
Stratus DDR5-5600 16 GB (2x8 GB) costs a little more.

### Will it work in a DDR4 board?

No. DDR5 modules are keyed differently and do not fit DDR4 slots, including the Meridian B760M
Forge D4 and all AM4 boards.

### Can I add a second kit later?

On a four-slot board, yes, but mixed kits are not guaranteed to work together, and with four
modules the officially supported speed drops to 3600 MT/s on AM5 and LGA1700 and 4400 MT/s on
LGA1851. Buying the capacity you need in one kit is the safer choice.
