## Overview

The Lodestar Q4 4 TB is an M.2 2280 NVMe SSD on PCIe 4.0, reading at up to 7000 MB/s and writing at
up to 6200 MB/s. It uses QLC flash to fit 4 TB onto a single M.2 drive at an affordable price.
Reads stay fast. During very long writes, after its cache fills, the write speed drops well below
the rated figure.

It is a great way to put a huge game library on fast storage, using only one M.2 slot, which
matters on boards with one or two slots.

## Who it's for

Gamers with large libraries and small-form-factor builders with only one or two M.2 slots who
want maximum capacity.

## Pros

- 4 TB in a single M.2 slot
- PCIe 4.0 read speed
- Ideal for boards with few M.2 slots

## Cons

- QLC flash: slower sustained writes after the cache fills
- Lower write endurance than TLC drives

## FAQ

### Is it good as a boot drive?

Yes. Everyday system use is mostly reads and short writes, which stay fast.

### Is QLC suitable for video editing?

For storing footage, yes. For writing large exports or recordings continuously, a TLC drive such
as the Arcwave A4 2 TB or the Arcwave A5 2 TB holds speed better.

### Will it fit my Mini-ITX board?

Yes. It fits any M-key M.2 slot, including both slots of the Kestrel B650E-I Perch.
