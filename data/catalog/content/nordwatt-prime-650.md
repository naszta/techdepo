## Overview

The Nordwatt Prime 650 is a 650 W ATX power supply with 80 PLUS Gold efficiency, fully modular
cables and a native 12V-2x6 (16-pin) cable for current graphics cards. It also has one 8-pin EPS,
two 8-pin PCIe and six SATA power connectors. It is 140 mm long and fits every ATX case we sell.

It is the most affordable way to power a 12V-2x6 card such as the Halcyon HX 7070 Twin. Watch the
headroom with high-power CPUs: a Voltaris V9 8950 with an HX 7070 leaves less than 20 % spare, and
the PC builder warns.

## Who it's for

Mid-range builders with an efficient CPU and a 12V-2x6 graphics card, and anyone who wants Gold
efficiency and modular cables on a budget.

## Pros

- Native 12V-2x6 cable
- Fully modular for tidy builds
- Gold efficiency runs cool and quiet
- Compact 140 mm length

## Cons

- One EPS connector only
- 650 W leaves little headroom with high-power CPUs and 250 W+ cards
- Only two PCIe 8-pin connectors

## FAQ

### Can it power the Halcyon HX 7070 Twin?

Yes. It has the native 12V-2x6 cable the card needs. With an efficient CPU such as the Voltaris V5
8600 there is plenty of headroom; with a high-power CPU such as the Voltaris V9 8950 or Helion H7
1470, choose the Prime 850.

### What is the difference to the Ferro Core 650?

Same wattage, but the Prime 650 adds a 12V-2x6 cable, Gold efficiency and fully modular cables.

### Can it power the Aurex Nova 80?

No. That card needs three 8-pin PCIe connectors and this unit has two.
