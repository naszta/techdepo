## Overview

The Breeze Glacier 120 is an all-in-one liquid cooler with a single 120 mm radiator and fan, rated
for 180 W. It includes mounts for AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. Its low pump block avoids height
limits, and the small radiator fits almost anywhere there is a 120 mm fan mount.

It fits every case we sell except the Tessel Sliver ITX, which has no radiator mounts. In the
Tessel Atlas Mid Tower it goes in the rear 120 mm mount, and in the Tessel Nook ITX on the side.

## Who it's for

Compact builds where a tower cooler is too tall and a 240 mm radiator does not fit.

## Pros

- Fits almost any 120 mm fan mount
- Low pump block avoids cooler height limits
- Mounts for every current socket

## Cons

- Rated for 180 W; a 240 mm AIO is quieter at the same load
- Pump adds a faint hum
- No radiator mount in the Tessel Sliver ITX

## FAQ

### Is it better than a tower air cooler?

Not at the same price. A Breeze Frost 120 cools similarly and is quieter. The Glacier 120 is for
cases where a tower cooler does not fit.

### Can it cool a Voltaris V9 8950?

Not for sustained loads. The V9 8950 draws up to about 230 W under all-core load, above the
Glacier 120's 180 W rating, so it would throttle and the PC builder warns. Choose a 240 mm or larger
AIO, such as the Breeze Glacier 240 (250 W).

### Does it need maintenance?

No. It is a sealed loop.
