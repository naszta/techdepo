## Overview

The Lodestar Glide M.2 SATA 1 TB is an M.2 2280 SSD that uses the SATA protocol instead of NVMe. It
reads at up to 550 MB/s and writes at up to 500 MB/s, the same as a 2.5-inch SATA SSD, but without
cables.

The important caveat: an M.2 SATA drive only works in an M.2 slot that supports SATA. Many modern
slots are NVMe only. On our boards, these slots accept it: the only slot of the Meridian A520M
Core, the second slot of the Kestrel B550 Glide, Kestrel B650M Wing and Meridian B760M Forge D4, the
third slot of the Kestrel B650 Aero and Kestrel B760 Vector, the fourth of the Meridian X870E Crest
and Meridian Z790 Crest, and the fifth of the Meridian Z890 Crest. The Meridian A620M Lite, Meridian
H610M Base, Kestrel B650E-I Perch and Kestrel B860I Hover have NVMe-only slots and cannot use it.

## Who it's for

Owners of older laptops and boards whose M.2 slots support SATA, who want a cable-free upgrade.

## Pros

- No SATA or power cables needed
- Works in older laptops and boards with SATA M.2 slots
- Affordable

## Cons

- Only works in M.2 slots that support SATA
- SATA speed, far behind NVMe drives at a similar price

## FAQ

### Will it work in my M.2 slot?

Only if the slot supports SATA. The product page of each board lists which of its M.2 slots do,
and the PC builder checks it.

### Should I buy this or an NVMe drive?

If your slot supports NVMe, an NVMe drive such as the Arcwave A3 1 TB is several times faster for
a similar price. This drive is for slots that only support SATA, or SATA as well.

### Does it use one of my SATA ports?

No. It sits in the M.2 slot, although on some boards using an M.2 slot in SATA mode can disable a
SATA port; check the board manual.
