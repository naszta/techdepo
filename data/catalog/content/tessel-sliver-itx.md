## Overview

The Tessel Sliver ITX is a slim Mini-ITX case designed to stand upright next to a monitor or lie
under a TV. Its slim profile sets strict limits: air coolers up to 58 mm tall, SFX power supplies up
to 100 mm long and graphics cards up to 285 mm long and two slots thick. There are no radiator
mounts, so AIO coolers do not fit. It has two 2.5-inch bays, no 3.5-inch bays and no included fans.

Within those limits it makes a surprisingly capable PC. A low-profile cooler such as the Breeze
Whisper LP (47 mm), an SFX unit such as the Ferro SF750 and a two-slot card such as the Halcyon HX
6060 Dual (242 mm) all fit.

## Who it's for

Builders who want the smallest, slimmest PC for a desk or living room and are happy to choose
parts carefully.

## Pros

- Very slim, living-room friendly design
- Takes two-slot graphics cards up to 285 mm
- Fits 100 mm SFX units with a native 12V-2x6 cable, such as the Ferro SF750

## Cons

- Air coolers limited to 58 mm; no AIO support
- SFX power supplies up to 100 mm only (no SFX-L)
- Hot CPUs need a lower power limit to stay quiet
- Graphics cards thicker than two slots do not fit

## FAQ

### Which CPU coolers fit?

Only low-profile coolers up to 58 mm, such as the Breeze Whisper LP (47 mm). The Coldmark Slim 67
is 67 mm and too tall, and there is no radiator mount for an AIO. The Whisper LP is rated for 95 W,
so it suits 65 W CPUs such as the Voltaris V5 8600.

### Does the Ferro SF850L fit?

No. It is an SFX-L unit, 130 mm long, and the Sliver takes 100 mm SFX units only. The Ferro SF600
and SF750 fit.

### Which graphics cards fit?

Two-slot cards up to 285 mm: the Aurex Nova 50, Halcyon HX 6050 Solo and Halcyon HX 6060 Dual.
Thicker 2.5-slot cards such as the Halcyon HX 6070 Dual and HX 7070 SFF do not fit, even though
they are short enough, and the Aurex Nova 70 (287 mm) is also 2 mm too long. For those, choose the
Tessel Nook ITX, which takes three-slot cards up to 300 mm.
