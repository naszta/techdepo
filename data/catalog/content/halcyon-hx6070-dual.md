## Overview

The Halcyon HX 6070 Dual is a 254 mm, 2.5-slot graphics card with 12 GB of VRAM for high-refresh
1440p gaming. It draws 220 W through two 8-pin PCIe connectors, and the vendor recommends a 650 W
power supply.

At 254 mm, it is one of the most powerful cards that still fits small cases such as the Tessel
Nook ITX (300 mm) and Lattice Pocket ITX (330 mm). In the Nook, pair it with an SFX unit that has
two 8-pin PCIe connectors, such as the Ferro SF750. The slim Tessel Sliver ITX takes two-slot cards
only, so this 2.5-slot card does not fit it.

## Who it's for

1440p gamers, and small-form-factor builders who want strong performance on classic 8-pin power.

## Pros

- Strong 1440p performance
- Compact 254 mm length
- Uses standard 8-pin PCIe connectors, no 12V-2x6 needed

## Cons

- Needs two 8-pin PCIe connectors
- 12 GB of VRAM is enough today but not generous

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

The vendor recommends 650 W with two 8-pin PCIe connectors. The Ferro Core 650 and Nordwatt Prime
650 both qualify; in SFX, the Ferro SF750.

### Does it fit a Mini-ITX case?

The Tessel Nook ITX and Lattice Pocket ITX, yes. The slim Tessel Sliver ITX takes two-slot cards
only, and this card is 2.5 slots thick.

### Does it need a 12V-2x6 cable?

No. It uses two standard 8-pin PCIe connectors.
