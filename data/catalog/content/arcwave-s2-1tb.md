## Overview

The Arcwave S2 1 TB is a 2.5-inch SATA SSD reading at up to 550 MB/s and writing at up to 510 MB/s.
It needs a SATA data cable to a free SATA port on the motherboard, a SATA power connector from the
power supply, and a 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch bay in the case.

Compared with a hard drive it boots and loads several times faster and is silent. Compared with
NVMe drives it is slower, but it does not use an M.2 slot, which makes it the easy way to add
storage to a board whose M.2 slots are full.

## Who it's for

Upgraders replacing an old hard drive, and builders adding storage to a board with no free M.2
slot.

## Pros

- Works in any PC with a free SATA port
- Silent and much faster than a hard drive
- Does not use an M.2 slot

## Cons

- SATA speed limit of about 550 MB/s
- Needs cables and a drive bay

## FAQ

### Does my case have room for it?

It fits a 2.5-inch bay or a 3.5-inch bay. Every case we sell has at least two 2.5-inch bays.

### How many SATA drives can I add?

As many as your board has SATA ports and your power supply has SATA power connectors. Mini-ITX
boards such as the Kestrel B650E-I Perch have two SATA ports.

### Is it fast enough for games?

Yes. Load times are a little longer than on NVMe, but far shorter than on a hard drive.
