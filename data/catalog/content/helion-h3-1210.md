## Overview

The Helion H3 1210 is a four-core, eight-thread LGA1700 processor from the Falcon generation. It
boosts to 4.3 GHz, has integrated graphics and comes with a stock cooler. Its 60 W TDP and about
89 W peak draw keep it cool and quiet.

Like every LGA1700 CPU, it works with both DDR4 and DDR5, but each board supports only one of
them: DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s on a DDR4 board such as the Meridian B760M Forge D4, or DDR5 up to
4800 MT/s on a DDR5 board such as the Kestrel B760 Vector. Falcon is the first LGA1700 family,
so every LGA1700 board supports it without a BIOS update.

## Who it's for

Office, school and home PCs, and starter gaming builds where the graphics card gets the budget.

## Pros

- Integrated graphics and a stock cooler: nothing else to buy
- Works with DDR4 or DDR5 boards
- Supported by every LGA1700 board out of the box
- Very low power draw

## Cons

- Four cores limit heavy multitasking and modern open-world games
- Official DDR5 speed is only 4800 MT/s

## FAQ

### Can I reuse my DDR4 memory?

Yes, on a DDR4 LGA1700 board such as the Meridian B760M Forge D4. DDR5 boards do not take DDR4
modules.

### Do I need a graphics card?

No. The integrated graphics handle the desktop, office work and video. Add a card for gaming.

### Can I upgrade later?

Usually. Most LGA1700 boards also take Harrier CPUs such as the Helion H5 1440F and H7 1470; check
that the board supports Harrier. The Meridian H610M Base is the exception: it runs Falcon CPUs
only.
