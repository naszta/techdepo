## Overview

The Lumen Pulse DDR5-6400 64 GB kit has two 32 GB modules rated for 6400 MT/s at CL32, with a 46 mm
RGB heat spreader. Two modules give 64 GB even on two-slot boards such as Mini-ITX boards and the
Meridian A620M Lite (whose 64 GB maximum it exactly matches), and they are easier on the memory
controller than four.

6400 MT/s is the official speed of Osprey (LGA1851) CPUs, so it runs at full speed there. On
Obsidian (AM5) and Harrier (LGA1700) it runs at 5600 MT/s by default, and 6400 MT/s with the
profile enabled on boards rated for it.

## Who it's for

Creators and power users who want 64 GB with room to spare, including small-form-factor builds.

## Pros

- 64 GB in just two modules
- Runs at rated speed on LGA1851
- Fits two-slot and Mini-ITX boards

## Cons

- 46 mm tall; check clearance under tower coolers
- Runs at the CPU's official speed (5600 MT/s on Obsidian and Harrier CPUs) by default
- Expensive

## FAQ

### Will it work in the Kestrel B650E-I Perch?

Yes. Two modules fit its two slots, and 64 GB is within its 96 GB maximum.

### Two modules or four?

Two. Two 32 GB modules usually run faster and more reliably than four 16 GB modules.

### Does it work with DDR4 boards?

No. It is DDR5 only.
