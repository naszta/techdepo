## Overview

The Voltaris V5 7600 is a six-core, twelve-thread processor from the first AM5 generation
(Quartz). It boosts to 5.1 GHz, runs DDR5 officially up to 5200 MT/s and has basic integrated
graphics. It also comes with a stock cooler, which makes it the cheapest complete way onto the
AM5 platform.

Because Quartz is the first AM5 family, every AM5 motherboard supports it with the BIOS it ships
with, including early and budget boards such as the Meridian A620M Lite. Faster DDR5 kits work
but run at 5200 MT/s unless you enable their memory profile.

## Who it's for

Budget gamers and office builders who want a modern DDR5 platform with an upgrade path, without
paying for a separate cooler.

## Pros

- Works in every AM5 board without a BIOS update
- Stock cooler included
- Integrated graphics as a display fallback
- Low 65 W TDP and about 88 W peak draw

## Cons

- Official memory speed is 5200 MT/s, lower than newer AM5 chips
- Integrated graphics are basic: fine for the desktop, not for gaming
- DDR5 only

## FAQ

### Does it need a BIOS update?

No. Quartz is the first AM5 family and every AM5 board supports it out of the box. That also
makes it useful for updating a board without BIOS flashback before installing an Obsidian CPU.

### Can I use DDR4 memory?

No. AM5 is DDR5 only, and DDR4 modules do not fit DDR5 slots.

### Is the stock cooler enough?

Yes, at stock settings. A tower cooler such as the Breeze Frost 120 makes the system quieter
under long loads.
