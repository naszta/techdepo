## Overview

The Coldmark Classic 92 is a compact single-tower air cooler with a 92 mm fan, 135 mm tall and
rated for 120 W. It is a cheap, quiet upgrade over most stock coolers for CPUs that draw up to
120 W under sustained load, such as the Voltaris V5 5600, V5 7600 and V5 8600.

Note the mounting kit: it supports AMD AM4 and AM5 only. It cannot be mounted on Intel LGA1700 or
LGA1851 boards, so Helion CPUs need a different cooler, such as the Breeze Frost 120.

## Who it's for

Budget AMD builders who want a quieter, cooler-running PC than the stock cooler gives, in almost
any tower case.

## Pros

- Very low price
- Compact 135 mm height fits most towers and the Lattice Pocket ITX
- Quieter than most stock coolers

## Cons

- AMD AM4 and AM5 only; no Intel mounts
- Rated for 120 W, too low for the Voltaris V7 5800, V7 8700 and V9 8950
- 92 mm fan gets louder under sustained load than 120 mm coolers

## FAQ

### Does it fit LGA1700 or LGA1851?

No. The mounting kit covers AM4 and AM5 only. For Helion CPUs, choose the Breeze Frost 120 or
another cooler with Intel mounts.

### Is it enough for the Voltaris V7 8700?

Not for sustained loads. The V7 8700 has a 120 W TDP but draws up to about 162 W under all-core
load, above this cooler's 120 W rating, so it would run hot and throttle and the PC builder warns.
The Breeze Frost 120 (180 W) is the right size.

### Does it fit small cases?

At 135 mm it fits every tower and the Lattice Pocket ITX (160 mm), but not the Tessel Nook ITX
(70 mm) or Tessel Sliver ITX (58 mm).
