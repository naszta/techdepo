## Overview

The Halcyon HX 6050 Solo is a 178 mm, two-slot, single-fan graphics card with 8 GB of VRAM for
1080p gaming. It draws 115 W through one 8-pin PCIe connector and the slot, and the vendor
recommends a 450 W power supply.

Its short length is the point: it fits the tightest Mini-ITX cases, including the Tessel Sliver
ITX, and leaves plenty of room for cables and airflow in larger ones.

## Who it's for

Small-form-factor builders and 1080p gamers on a budget who want a real gaming card in a tiny
case.

## Pros

- Only 178 mm long
- Single 8-pin connector and modest 115 W draw
- Solid 1080p performance

## Cons

- Single fan gets louder under sustained load than dual-fan cards
- 8 GB of VRAM limits texture settings in some new games

## FAQ

### What power supply do I need?

A 450 W unit with one 8-pin PCIe connector is enough for a typical system. Every power supply we
sell qualifies.

### Will it fit the Tessel Sliver ITX?

Yes. The Sliver takes cards up to 285 mm and the HX 6050 Solo is 178 mm long.

### How does it compare to the HX 6060 Dual?

The HX 6060 Dual is faster and quieter under load but 242 mm long. Choose the Solo when length is
the limit.
