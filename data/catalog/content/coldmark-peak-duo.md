## Overview

The Coldmark Peak Duo is a high-performance dual-tower air cooler rated for 270 W, with mounts for
AM4, AM5, LGA1700 and LGA1851. Its performance rivals 280 mm AIOs, with no pump to wear out.

The catch is its 168 mm height. It fits cases with at least 168 mm of clearance: the Tessel Cube M
(170 mm), Lattice Flow 500 (175 mm) and Tessel Summit Full Tower (185 mm). It does not fit the
Tessel Atlas Mid Tower (165 mm), the Lattice Glass 600 (167 mm), or any of our budget or
small-form-factor cases.

## Who it's for

Enthusiasts with a high-power CPU and a roomy case who want the best air cooling available.

## Pros

- Rated for 270 W
- Rivals 280 mm AIO performance
- No pump, no leaks, long life

## Cons

- 168 mm tall: fits only a few cases
- Heavy
- Can overhang tall memory modules

## FAQ

### Does it fit the Tessel Atlas Mid Tower?

No. The Atlas allows 165 mm and the Peak Duo is 168 mm. The Breeze Frost 140 Duo (158 mm) fits, or
use a 360 mm AIO in the front mount.

### Can it cool a Helion H7 1470?

Yes, very well. It is rated for 270 W, above the H7 1470's 253 W sustained draw.

### Will it clear tall memory?

Memory up to 36 mm fits under the front fan, including every Nimbus DDR5 kit (32 to 34 mm). With
taller kits, raise the front fan; the cooler then stands taller than 168 mm, and the PC builder
checks it against the case.
