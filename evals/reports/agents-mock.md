## Judge and reply confidence

The judge scores every AI text that passed the guardrails (the lowest of its three `P(yes)`; with `p(intent)` = 1.0 that is the reply confidence). *Good* texts passed every check of their scenario, *bad* ones did not. `send_above_confidence` sends a text whose confidence reaches the threshold and holds the rest for review.

**Mock backend: this section measures nothing.** The offline judge gives every grounded, polite draft about the same scores (0.85–0.90) whether its scenario passed or not, so good and bad texts overlap and the suggested threshold below is arbitrary.
